How this works

  1. Choose a poster style and an SRK film. The film's King Name becomes your poster's title.
  2. Be the King, or be with the King. Be with the King keeps the King's face as it is, and you take another face on the poster.
  3. Add up to 3 photos: a selfie, or a group photo with friends. The site finds every face and labels it A, B, C. Tap a face to leave it out, or tap a missed face to add it.
  4. Who goes where: the poster's faces are numbered 1, 2, 3. For each number, tap the letter of the face that goes there, or "Keep original" to leave that face as it is. With the King, tap "👑 King" on his face.
  5. Make my poster. The AI paints your faces into the poster, only where you chose, in the poster's own style. Every other face and the rest of the poster stay exactly as they are. Your photos are not kept.
  6. Share it and earn STARKS.
