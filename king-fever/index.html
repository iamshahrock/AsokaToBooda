KING Fever

INQUILAAB, KING KE SAATH!
#FreeMyFace — A REAL REVOLUTION BY FANS BEGINS

The makers of AI generators have built and trained models using human creativity, celebrity faces and performances without seeking permission from the people whose identities they exploit. Now they are monetising those faces, turning actors into raw material for machines and fans into unwilling spectators of an AI-generated free-for-all.

No rules. No rights. No permission. Just profit.

This anthem is the fans’ answer. No bullets. No bloodshed. Just pens, voices and art.

The demand is simple: respect the actor’s face, give fans their due, and make the face-sellers pay. No technology company should get a free pass simply because its product is powered by AI.

From every corner of the world, one voice rises in defiance:

THE KING’S FACE. THE FANS’ VOICE. EVERY ARTIST’S RIGHT.

This is an open-source Fan Anthem. Use it freely. Create your own audio or video version. Add your voice, your art, your language, and submit it.
