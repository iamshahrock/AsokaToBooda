The class of '65

Robert Downey Jr. was born on 4 April 1965. Shah Rukh Khan was born on 2 November 1965. Same year, two hemispheres, two careers that went from boy wonder to written off to the biggest comebacks in their industries. Both once saved the world in a superhero suit, one red, one blue. This Christmas, at 61, both walk to the dark side of the screen: one as the villain of the Marvel universe, one as the man they call KING.
