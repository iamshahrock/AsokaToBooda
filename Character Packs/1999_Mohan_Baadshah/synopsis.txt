Baadshah (1999) — Expanded story synopsis

Raj, a small-time private detective who calls himself Baadshah, dreams of becoming a celebrated investigator. A routine assignment draws him into a plot involving a political assassination, a wealthy industrialist, and a dangerous conspiracy. Through a chain of mistaken identities and elaborate deceptions, Raj is mistaken for a highly trained international agent. He improvises his way through threats, using bluff, comic ingenuity, and help from people he meets along the way. The case forces the amateur detective to become more courageous than his self-image suggests, while the story mixes action, romance, and spoof-like humor.

Character lens: Raj's showmanship masks a quick-thinking survivor who discovers genuine bravery.
