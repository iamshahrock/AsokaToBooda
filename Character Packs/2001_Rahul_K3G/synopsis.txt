Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham (2001) — Expanded story synopsis

Rahul Raichand, the adopted son of a wealthy and tradition-bound family, falls in love with Anjali, whose lively middle-class household differs sharply from his father's expectations. When Rahul marries Anjali against his father's wishes, he is disowned and leaves India. Years later, his younger brother Rohan learns the truth and travels abroad, determined to reunite the family. Rohan's efforts bring buried grief, pride, and affection to the surface. The story centers on the tension between parental authority and personal choice, and on whether love can repair a family fractured by status and wounded pride.

Character lens: Rahul is torn between filial devotion and the responsibility to protect the family he chooses.
