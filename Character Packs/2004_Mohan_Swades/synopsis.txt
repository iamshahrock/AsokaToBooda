Swades (2004) — Expanded story synopsis

Mohan Bhargava, an Indian-American scientist working at NASA, returns to India to bring his childhood caretaker, Kaveri Amma, to the United States. His visit to a rural village exposes him to the everyday barriers facing its residents, including unreliable electricity, limited opportunity, and entrenched social divisions. Through his relationships with a village teacher, children, and local families, Mohan begins to question the distance between technical achievement and social responsibility. Rather than simply completing his original errand, he becomes involved in a community-led effort to improve local infrastructure and ultimately reconsiders where he belongs.

Character lens: Mohan's transformation is from individual professional success toward service, participation, and a chosen sense of home.
