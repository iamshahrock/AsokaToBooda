Karan Arjun (1995) — Expanded story synopsis

Brothers Karan and Arjun are murdered by a powerful landlord after standing up to his cruelty. Their mother, Durga, refuses to believe that death has ended their bond and prays for their return. The brothers are reborn in different circumstances, growing up without conscious memory of their former lives. As events bring them into contact with the people and places tied to their deaths, fragments of memory and instinct awaken. Durga recognizes them and helps restore their shared purpose. Together, they confront the man responsible and pursue justice across the boundary between life, death, and rebirth.

Character lens: Arjun’s loyalty and courage complement Karan’s force; the mother’s faith is the emotional engine of the reincarnation story.
