Pardes (1997) — Expanded story synopsis

Kishorilal, a wealthy Indian expatriate, hopes to connect his son Rajiv with Indian traditions and arranges his marriage to Ganga, a young woman from his hometown. He asks his trusted associate Arjun to bring Ganga to America and help the couple become acquainted. During their journey and time together, Arjun and Ganga develop a bond grounded in shared values and emotional honesty. Rajiv’s behavior reveals a troubling divide between outward privilege and personal character. Arjun must confront his loyalty to Kishorilal and his own feelings, while Ganga decides what kind of life and partnership she can accept.

Character lens: Arjun is caught between duty and conscience; Ganga’s integrity anchors the clash between cultural image and lived values.
