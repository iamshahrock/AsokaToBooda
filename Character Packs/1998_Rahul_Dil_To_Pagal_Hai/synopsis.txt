Dil To Pagal Hai (1997) — Expanded story synopsis

Rahul is a choreographer who believes that somewhere there is one destined partner for everyone, while his close friend Nisha quietly loves him. When Rahul’s leading dancer is injured, he casts Pooja, a gifted performer who is engaged to another man. Rehearsals and shared artistic work draw Rahul and Pooja together, even as Nisha’s feelings and Pooja’s commitment complicate the developing romance. The film stages love as both an idea and an experience: destiny may be imagined, but choices and emotional honesty determine whether people can act on it.

Character lens: Rahul’s certainty about an ideal love is tested by the real, complicated person who enters his life.

Folder note: Film released in 1997, not 1998.
