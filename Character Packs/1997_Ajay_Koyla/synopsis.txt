Koyla (1997) — Expanded story synopsis

Shankar is a mute laborer forced to serve the brutal Raja, who controls a remote community through fear. Raja arranges a marriage to a young woman, Gauri, while concealing his cruelty and true intentions. Shankar becomes Gauri’s unlikely ally, helping her escape and revealing the violence underpinning Raja’s authority. Their flight turns into a struggle for survival through harsh terrain, with the powerful ruler pursuing them. Shankar’s silence, initially used by others to deny him agency, becomes part of a larger story about dignity, courage, and resistance to oppression.

Character lens: Shankar communicates through action; his quiet endurance develops into fierce protection and rebellion.
