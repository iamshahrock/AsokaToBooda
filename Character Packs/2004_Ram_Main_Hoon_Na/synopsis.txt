Main Hoon Na (2004) — Expanded story synopsis

Major Ram Prasad Sharma is assigned to protect a general's daughter and secretly reunite with his estranged half-brother, who attends the same college. To carry out the mission, Ram enrolls as a student and navigates campus life while concealing his military identity. A peace initiative between India and Pakistan is threatened by a rogue officer and his armed followers, whose plan endangers students and civilians. Ram’s two missions converge: he must protect the people around him, repair his family bond, and prevent a violent attack. The film blends campus comedy, action, romance, and a message of reconciliation.

Character lens: Ram combines disciplined service with a longing for family acceptance.
