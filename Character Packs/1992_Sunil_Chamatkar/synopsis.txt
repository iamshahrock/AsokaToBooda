Chamatkar (1992) — Expanded story synopsis

Sunder, an idealistic but unlucky young man, arrives in Mumbai hoping to build a future through cricket. After being cheated and left stranded, he encounters a ghost, Marco, who asks for help resolving the injustice surrounding his death. Their unlikely partnership turns Sunder’s life into a comic supernatural adventure. As Sunder investigates the past and grows close to a woman connected to the mystery, he must balance his own ambitions with his promise to help Marco find peace. The story blends friendship, romance, sports aspirations, and a battle against powerful wrongdoing.

Character lens: Sunder begins as a naïve dreamer and becomes more resourceful through loyalty and moral courage.
