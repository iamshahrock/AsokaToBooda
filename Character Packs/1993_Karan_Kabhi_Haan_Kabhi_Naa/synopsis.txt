Kabhi Haan Kabhi Naa (1994 release) — Expanded story synopsis

Sunil is a charming but unreliable young musician who dreams of success and is deeply in love with Anna, a member of his band. Anna loves Chris, and Sunil’s attempts to win her affection lead him into lies, manipulation, and increasingly uncomfortable situations. His schemes sometimes hurt the very people he wants to impress. When the truth emerges, Sunil must face disappointment and accept that love cannot be earned through deception. The film closes on a bittersweet note: he does not get the romantic outcome he wanted, but gains a measure of honesty and emotional maturity.
