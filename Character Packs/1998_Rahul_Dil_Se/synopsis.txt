Dil Se.. (1998) — Expanded story synopsis

Amar, a radio journalist, meets a mysterious woman named Meghna while reporting in a conflict-affected region. Fascinated by her, he pursues her across India despite her repeated refusals and the secrets she guards. As Amar’s personal obsession deepens, he discovers that Meghna is connected to a militant organization preparing a major attack. The relationship unfolds against political violence, alienation, and the human costs of extremism. Amar tries to reach Meghna as a person beyond the cause that has consumed her, but the story moves toward a tragic collision between private longing and a larger mission.

Character lens: Amar’s passionate persistence is both romantic and troubling; the film frames love against the machinery of political violence.
