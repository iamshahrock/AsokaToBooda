One 2 Ka 4 (2001) — Expanded story synopsis

Arun Verma is a police officer whose partner is killed during a criminal operation, leaving behind four children. Arun takes responsibility for the children while continuing his work and struggling to balance law enforcement with an improvised family life. His colleague and friend Geeta becomes increasingly involved in their household and in Arun’s emotional world. A case involving drug trafficking and police corruption complicates the investigation, putting the children and the adults around them at risk. The film combines buddy-cop action with domestic comedy and asks whether responsibility can turn an isolated professional into a dependable parent.

Character lens: Arun’s brash confidence gives way to care, sacrifice, and emotional attachment.
