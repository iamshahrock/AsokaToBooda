Deewana (1992) — Expanded story synopsis

Raja is a young man whose life is transformed when he falls in love with Kajal, a woman already married to the celebrated singer Ravi. Their marriage is threatened by a violent family conspiracy, and Ravi is believed to have been killed. As Kajal tries to rebuild her life, Raja becomes her protector and eventually marries her. But the past returns when Ravi, who survived the attack, reappears. The reunion exposes the lies and ambitions that separated the couple and forces the characters to confront loyalty, identity, and the difference between love freely chosen and love imposed by circumstance.

Character lens: Raja is an impulsive romantic whose courage develops into responsibility. The story moves from musical romance into melodrama and suspense.
