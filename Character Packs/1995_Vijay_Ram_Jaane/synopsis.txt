Ram Jaane (1995) — Expanded story synopsis

A boy abandoned as a child grows up without a stable identity or moral anchor. Known as Ram Jaane, he returns to the neighborhood where he was raised and becomes involved in its criminal economy. His childhood friend Murli has chosen social work and tries to guide young people away from violence, while Bela represents a possible life beyond the streets. Ram Jaane is torn between loyalty to the gang that gives him belonging and the possibility of becoming someone different. As criminal rivalries intensify, his choices expose the costs of a life built on intimidation and the difficulty of escaping a role society has assigned him.

Character lens: Ram Jaane is a volatile, wounded outsider whose longing for acceptance competes with the violence he has learned.
