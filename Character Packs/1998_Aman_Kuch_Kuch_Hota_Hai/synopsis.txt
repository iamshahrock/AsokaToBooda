Kuch Kuch Hota Hai (1998) — Expanded story synopsis

College friends Rahul and Anjali share an easy, teasing closeness, but Anjali realizes she loves Rahul just as he falls for Tina. Anjali leaves, and Rahul and Tina marry and have a daughter, also named Anjali. Years later, Tina’s letters guide the child toward reuniting her father with his old friend. The younger Anjali sets out to bring Rahul and the now-grown Anjali together, while old feelings and new commitments complicate the plan. The story moves between youthful friendship and adult grief, asking whether love can change form over time and whether people can recognize the person who has always been beside them.

Character lens: Rahul matures from a self-absorbed college flirt into a father confronting loss, memory, and the depth of friendship.
