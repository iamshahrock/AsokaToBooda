Army (1996) — Expanded story synopsis

Major Arjun Singh is a decorated army officer whose life is cut short after he confronts a powerful criminal. His widow, Geeta, is left grieving and faces a system unable to deliver justice. She brings together other women who have suffered at the hands of the same man, and their shared grief becomes a plan for retaliation. The group trains and acts together, blurring the line between justice and revenge. Arjun’s memory and the moral authority associated with his service remain central to Geeta’s determination, even as the women must confront the consequences of taking justice into their own hands.

Character lens: Arjun is the absent moral catalyst; Geeta carries the story’s active transformation from bereavement to resistance.
