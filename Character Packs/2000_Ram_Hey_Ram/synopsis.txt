Hey Ram (2000) — Expanded story synopsis

This folder's role attribution is mistaken: Shah Rukh Khan appears as Amjad Ali Khan in Kamal Haasan's historical drama, not as a character named Ram. The story follows Saket Ram, whose life is shattered by communal violence during Partition. Grief and rage draw him toward a conspiracy to assassinate Mahatma Gandhi. As he travels through a fractured subcontinent and encounters people of different faiths, his certainty is challenged by compassion and the consequences of hatred. Amjad, a close friend whose Muslim identity places him in danger, is central to the film's exploration of friendship, communalism, memory, and moral responsibility.

Character lens: Amjad represents humane friendship amid escalating communal suspicion.

Folder note: This is not a Shah Rukh Khan-led film; its central protagonist is Saket Ram, played by Kamal Haasan.
