King Uncle (1993) — Expanded story synopsis

Anil Bansal is a wealthy, strict businessman who believes discipline and money can solve nearly every problem. His rigid worldview is challenged when a spirited orphan, Munna, enters his life and asks him to help reunite her with her siblings. Through the children and the people around them, Anil is confronted with the emotional damage caused by his own hardness and the value of affection, trust, and family. The plot follows his gradual transformation from an emotionally distant authority figure into someone willing to use his resources and influence to protect vulnerable people.

Character lens: Anil’s arc is a movement from control and material success toward tenderness and chosen family.
