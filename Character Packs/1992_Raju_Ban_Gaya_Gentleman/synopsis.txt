Raju Ban Gaya Gentleman (1992) — Expanded story synopsis

Raj Mathur, an ambitious engineering graduate from a small town, comes to Bombay determined to make a name for himself. His talent earns him a place in a construction company, where professional opportunity brings him into contact with wealth, influence, and competing loyalties. As Raj becomes successful, he is drawn between the people who supported his beginnings and the glamorous world he hopes to enter. A major development project reveals the human cost of corporate ambition and forces him to decide what success should mean. The story follows a young professional’s coming-of-age, testing whether integrity can survive the pursuit of status.

Character lens: Raj is energetic and idealistic, with ambition that must mature into ethical responsibility.
