Devdas (2002) — Expanded story synopsis

Devdas Mukherjee returns from England expecting to marry his childhood companion Paro, but his wealthy family rejects the match on grounds of class and status. Paro is married to another man, and Devdas, unable to face his loss, turns to alcohol and self-destruction. Chandramukhi, a courtesan, sees his suffering and offers care that he struggles to accept. As Devdas spirals, pride and emotional paralysis prevent him from making meaningful choices. The story becomes a tragic portrait of love defeated not simply by social barriers but by a protagonist's inability to act with courage when it matters.

Character lens: Devdas is a romantic tragic figure whose privilege cannot protect him from the consequences of passivity.
