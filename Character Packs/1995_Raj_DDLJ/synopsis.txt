Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (1995) — Expanded story synopsis

Raj Malhotra and Simran Singh meet while travelling through Europe. Their teasing friendship develops into love, but Simran is already promised in marriage to a man chosen by her traditional father, Baldev. Raj returns to India and follows Simran’s family to Punjab, determined not to elope or force her to abandon her family. Instead, he tries to earn Baldev’s trust and win consent. The conflict becomes a negotiation between individual choice and inherited duty. Raj’s patience and Simran’s growing resolve lead toward a final test of whether love can be recognized without severing family bonds.

Character lens: Raj’s playful confidence matures into patience and respect for Simran’s agency; Simran must find the courage to speak for her own future.
