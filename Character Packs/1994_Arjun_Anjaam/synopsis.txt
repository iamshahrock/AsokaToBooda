Anjaam (1994) — Expanded story synopsis

Vijay Agnihotri, a wealthy and possessive man, becomes obsessed with Shivani, an airline employee who rejects his advances and marries another man. Unable to accept her autonomy, Vijay uses his influence and wealth to harass and destroy her family life. Shivani suffers devastating losses and is imprisoned, but refuses to surrender her agency. The conflict develops into a grim cycle of obsession, injustice, and retaliation, with Shivani fighting to survive and seek accountability. The film presents the consequences of entitlement and coercive control through a melodramatic revenge thriller.

Character lens: Vijay’s outward privilege conceals a destructive inability to accept rejection; Shivani’s endurance drives the narrative.
