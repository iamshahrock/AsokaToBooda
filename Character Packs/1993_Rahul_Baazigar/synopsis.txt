Baazigar (1993) — Expanded story synopsis

Ajay Sharma, living under an assumed identity as Vicky Malhotra, carries a deep grievance against a powerful businessman whose actions destroyed his family. He enters the businessman’s world and deliberately courts his daughters as part of a calculated revenge plan. His relationship with the younger daughter becomes emotionally complicated, while the elder daughter’s death and the inconsistencies around it raise suspicion. As the investigation closes in, Ajay’s carefully controlled double life begins to unravel. The thriller explores revenge, deception, ambition, and the moral cost of turning personal pain into a campaign of destruction.

Character lens: Ajay is a strategic, wounded antihero whose intelligence and charm are inseparable from his capacity for cruelty.
