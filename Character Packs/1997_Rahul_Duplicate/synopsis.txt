Duplicate (1998) — Expanded story synopsis

Bablu Chaudhry is a cheerful aspiring chef whose appearance is identical to that of Manu, a feared underworld criminal. When Manu escapes custody, the resemblance draws Bablu into a dangerous case of mistaken identity. Police, gangsters, and people from both men’s lives repeatedly confuse one for the other. Bablu must survive threats while trying to prove who he is, and Manu exploits the confusion to advance his own plans. The comedy of mistaken identity gradually becomes a contest between an ordinary man’s decency and a criminal’s self-preservation.

Character lens: Bablu’s warmth and improvisation contrast with Manu’s menace; the dual role turns identity into the film’s central comic engine.

Folder note: Duplicate was released in 1998, not 1997.
