Chalte Chalte (2003) — Expanded story synopsis

Raj Mathur, an independent transport entrepreneur, meets Priya, a successful fashion designer engaged to another man. Their connection grows into love, and they marry despite differences in temperament and background. The early romance gives way to financial pressure, professional setbacks, jealousy, and repeated arguments. Raj's pride and impulsiveness clash with Priya's expectations and ambitions, threatening the relationship they fought to build. As the marriage approaches collapse, both must decide whether love means winning an argument or learning to listen, change, and share responsibility.

Character lens: Raj's entrepreneurial energy is also a source of stubbornness; his emotional growth depends on humility and accountability.
