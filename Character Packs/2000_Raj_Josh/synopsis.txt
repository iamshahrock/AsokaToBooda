Josh (2000) — Expanded synopsis

In Goa, Max leads a close-knit gang of young men whose rivalry with a neighboring group is tied to neighborhood identity and old resentments. Max’s twin sister Shirley falls in love with Rahul, the younger brother of the rival gang’s leader. Their relationship threatens to turn a long-standing feud into a personal crisis. Max must choose between loyalty to his friends and his sister’s right to love whom she chooses. As confrontations escalate, the young people are forced to consider whether inherited hostility is worth the futures it endangers.

Character lens: Max is fiercely loyal and charismatic, but his protective instincts can perpetuate the cycle he wants to control.
