Oh Darling! Yeh Hai India! (1995) — Expanded story synopsis

An aspiring performer known as Hero dreams of making it in the film world. During an eventful night in Bombay, he and a young woman become entangled in a bizarre plot involving a corrupt figure who intends to seize power and manipulate the country’s political future. Their attempt to stop the scheme unfolds as an irreverent satire of celebrity culture, political opportunism, and national spectacle. The film uses heightened theatricality, parody, and absurd situations to question the gap between the ideals a nation advertises and the behavior of those who claim to represent it.

Character lens: Hero is an idealistic outsider whose romantic adventure becomes a satirical confrontation with power.
