KING's last lap: three days on a Formula One circuit, then the trailer

Shah Rukh Khan and Siddharth Anand began a three-day shoot at the Formula One track outside Delhi on Wednesday, with the whole circuit blocked for the film, Pinkvilla reported. The makers plan to wrap KING by the end of November; more songs and the trailer are expected after 23 November.

KING's final schedule has moved to a racetrack. According to Pinkvilla's report of 18 November, the makers have taken the entire Formula One circuit near Delhi for three days for a high-speed set piece that Siddharth Anand and his international action team spent weeks planning and rehearsing. The sequence is being shot on the real track rather than against green screens, a source told the outlet, to show Shah Rukh Khan in a raw new avatar. TV9 Hindi placed the location about 50 km from Delhi; Moneycontrol, Live Hindustan and others carried the report.

Ramayana crosses ₹1,000 crore gross in India in 14 days

Ramayana made ₹23.20 crore net in India on its second Wednesday and took its India gross to ₹1,009.05 crore, only the sixth Indian film to cross that mark, as reported by Sacnilk. Worldwide it stands at ₹1,519.05 crore.

Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana, with Ranbir Kapoor, Yash and Sai Pallavi, collected ₹23.20 crore net across India on Wednesday, its fourteenth day, down 18.9% from Tuesday, according to Sacnilk's figures updated at 10:16 PM IST. That lifted its India gross to ₹1,009.05 crore. Sacnilk reports that only five Indian films had crossed ₹1,000 crore gross in India before it, among them Baahubali 2, Pushpa 2, KGF Chapter 2 and the Dhurandhar films.
