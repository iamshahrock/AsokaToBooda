Ramayana: Part 1 made ₹53.50 crore on Thursday and has ₹1,033.55 crore gross worldwide after eight days, according to Sacnilk, which calls it the 11th Indian film past the mark.

Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana: Part 1 collected ₹53.50 crore net in India on Thursday 12 November, its eighth day, from 24,042 shows, according to Sacnilk's figures updated at 10:19 PM IST. That is 24.3% below Wednesday's ₹70.65 crore. The eight-day total is ₹564.60 crore net in India and ₹1,033.55 crore gross worldwide. Sacnilk reports that Ramayana is the 11th Indian film to cross ₹1,000 crore worldwide, and that it did so in eight days.
