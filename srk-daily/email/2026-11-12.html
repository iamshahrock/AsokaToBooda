Dubai crowns the King: a tower in his name, Gauri's penthouses sold out, and KING on stage

Shah Rukh Khan launched Shahrukhz Residences by Danube in Dubai on Wednesday night, with his sister and Gauri Khan's whole family in the arena. Gauri's penthouses sold out on launch day, Karan Johar hosted, and the King danced to Tu Mil Jaaye live for the first time.

On Wednesday night the Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai filled for a building. Not a film, not a concert: a 60-storey waterfront tower in Dubai Maritime City that carries Shah Rukh Khan's name. Shahrukhz Residences by Danube is the second tower the developer has named after him, and by the time he walked on stage, the evening had already become a family affair, a friendship and a film promotion, all at once.
