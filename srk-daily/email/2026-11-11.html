Gaiety-Galaxy, the G7 multiplex in Bandra, is working to reopen in the last week of November, Mid-Day reported on Tuesday 10 November. The theatre has been shut since 7 September. According to the paper, its management does not want to miss two of December's biggest Hindi releases: Salman Khan's Maatrubhumi on 4 December and KING on 24 December.
