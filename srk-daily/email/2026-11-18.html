A month before release, Avengers: Doomsday sells ₹10 crore of opening-day tickets in India

Marvel opened Indian bookings for Avengers: Doomsday on the night of 16 November, 30 days before its 17 December release, and sold about one lakh tickets at the three big multiplex chains in 19 hours, according to Sacnilk. KING, a week behind it, has not opened bookings or released a trailer.

The first shows went on sale after midnight on Monday night at multiplexes in Mumbai, among them PVR Lower Parel and Miraj Wadala, and by Tuesday night Avengers: Doomsday had crossed ₹10 crore gross in opening-day advance bookings in India. About one lakh tickets were sold across PVR, INOX and Cinepolis within roughly 19 hours, Sacnilk reported at 9:47 PM on Tuesday.
