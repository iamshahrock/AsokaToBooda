Ramayana's second Monday: ₹32 crore, and ₹1,437.25 crore worldwide

Ramayana: Part 1 fell 57.3% on its second Monday but stands at ₹792.60 crore net in India and ₹1,437.25 crore gross worldwide after 12 days, according to Sacnilk, which says 2026 is now the highest-grossing year ever at the Indian box office.
