"Peace is a decision": Shah Rukh Khan at the Global Peace Honours

At the Gateway of India on Friday night, Shah Rukh Khan said we spend our lives fighting, for our dreams, our identity, our own people, even a parking spot, but peace is the one thing we never need to fight for. He told the story of a question from AbRam, and ended with a verse about bowing your head with honour.

'Shah Rukh Khan's most important message at the Global Peace Honours 2026', AHRAR on YouTube, 21 Nov 2026. Tap the picture to watch.
He arrived late. "Sorry, I was a little late," he told the hosts, Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis, Eknath Shinde and Amruta Fadnavis, whose Divyaj Foundation runs the Global Peace Honours. "But traffic ka kuch karenge thode din ke andar." (We'll do something about the traffic in a few days.)
