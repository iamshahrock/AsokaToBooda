On Wednesday night Variety placed Shah Rukh Khan at No. 92 on its critics' list of 'The 100 Greatest Movie Stars of All Time', the only Indian on it, with Devdas as the film to see. By Thursday the ranking was everywhere: NDTV, The Indian Express, The Hollywood Reporter India, ETV Bharat, the Free Press Journal, IMDb's news page and Bangladesh's Daily Observer all carried it. Several led on who he had finished ahead of, Zendaya and Joaquin Phoenix among them; one Indian site, Abstract AF!, argued instead that the list forgets most of the world. TIME published its first TIME100 Next India on Thursday, and Aryan Khan, 29, is on it. His entry is written by the filmmaker and composer Vishal Bhardwaj, who recalls meeting him at private screenings in Mumbai and expecting the son of a legend to carry some entitlement; instead, he writes, he found a gentle, humble young man. He calls Aryan's creative voice very different from his father's but equally instinctive.
