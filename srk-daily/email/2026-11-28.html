At House of Instagram, Shah Rukh Khan launches Daddy Cool and talks of a 'kingdom of love'

The song was unveiled on stage in Mumbai before more than 700 creators. In conversation with Karan Johar, Shah Rukh Khan gave KING's Instagram bio as a line from the film.

Shah Rukh Khan unveiled Daddy Cool at House of Instagram 2026, Meta's flagship event in Mumbai, on Friday evening. Bollywood Hungama reports that more than 700 content creators from across the country attended. He sat down with filmmaker Karan Johar, watched creators' reels made to Tu Mil Jaaye, and called a few fans on stage, the Indian Express reports.
