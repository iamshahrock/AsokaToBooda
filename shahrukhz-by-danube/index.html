On Wednesday night the Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai filled for a building. Not a film, not a concert: a 60-storey waterfront tower in Dubai Maritime City that carries Shah Rukh Khan's name. Shahrukhz Residences by Danube is the second tower Danube has named after him, built by Rizwan Sajan. Gauri Khan designed its penthouses, and they sold out within hours. Karan Johar hosted the night on his own couch. And before the evening was over, Shah Rukh Khan brought KING to Dubai.

It was a family night too. Shah Rukh Khan's sister, Shehnaz Lala Rukh Khan, and Gauri Khan's entire family were in the arena to celebrate the launch, a historic one for the family: the second tower in his name, and penthouses in her design. The first was Shahrukhz by Danube, a 55-storey commercial office tower on Sheikh Zayed Road, launched on 9 December 2025 at the Dubai Exhibition Centre, where all of it, worth AED 2.1 billion, sold out on launch day.

KING comes to Dubai

Before he sat down on the couch, he danced. Shah Rukh Khan performed Tu Mil Jaaye, KING's first song, released on his 61st birthday on 2 November. Fan clubs on X called it the song's first live performance, and the clips were everywhere by midnight: on Instagram, the most-played video from the night had nearly half a million plays by morning. We read 104 Instagram posts about the launch: 4,493,209 plays and 217,194 likes between them, in about 12 hours.

By Thursday morning, Tu Mil Jaaye had passed 74.8 million views on YouTube (74,844,434 views and 739,100 likes, read 12 Nov 09:17 IST). KING opens in cinemas on 24 December, in the UAE as well, Gulf News reported.

The man who built it

The tower was built by Rizwan Sajan, the owner of Danube, and his own story would make a Shah Rukh Khan film. He was born in Mumbai in 1963 and grew up in Ghatkopar. He lost his father at 16 and left school to work, selling books, delivering milk and newspapers, and selling firecrackers at Diwali, Business Today reported. A job in building materials took him to Kuwait; the Gulf War sent him home. In the early 1990s a friend helped him to Dubai, where he started trading hardware. That trading business became Danube Group, today one of Dubai's big home builders. Reports put his own worth at about $2.5 billion.

Wednesday was their reunion, eleven months after the first tower, and on stage the two played it like old friends. Asked by Karan Johar what he would take from the actor, Sajan picked the dimples: with those, he said, he would sell twice as much property. Shah Rukh Khan's answer: then who would watch his films? Sajan also told the arena the advice the actor once gave him: you build the tower, leave the show to me.

Gauri's penthouses

The penthouses at the top of the tower are Gauri Khan's. She designed them with her team in India, and on stage she called the project "so personal." Danube's Adel Sajan, Rizwan's son, said she sat in on every Zoom call and worked through every detail, down to the materials and the corners of each room. Bringing her in was his father's idea.

All of her penthouses, valued at ₹50 crore each, sold out within hours, Gulf Good News reported, and fan clubs at the arena posted that they were gone before the evening began.

"Gauri, what a home you've made."Shah Rukh Khan, after telling the arena that at home Gauri decides everything, from the Wi-Fi to the chandeliers (Khaleej Times)

Karan Johar, on his own couch

Karan Johar hosted the night, and he brought his couch. The man who directed Shah Rukh Khan in Kuch Kuch Hota Hai, Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham, Kabhi Alvida Naa Kehna and My Name Is Khan ran the evening like an episode of his own talk show: an introduction for Gauri, a rapid-fire round for Sajan, and the questions that turned the night from property to life.

On that couch, Shah Rukh Khan talked about houses. Gulf News reported that he traced the wish back to a childhood without the home he wanted, and called buying his first home, and later Mannat, two of the least practical decisions of his life. Mannat is now a stop for fans and tourists in Bandra. Now there is an address in Dubai with his name on it. He called that "extremely humbling."

"My biggest yearning in life was to have a house."Shah Rukh Khan (Khaleej Times)
