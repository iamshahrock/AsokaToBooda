Chapter 38 · DOOM to KING

[image: DOOM to KING: One Christmas, Two Crowns]

DOOM to KING: One Christmas, Two Crowns. (The author’s art for the campaign, agarmainkinghota.com)

Avengers: Doomsday opens on 18 December 2026, with Robert Downey Jr. back at Marvel as its villain, Doctor Doom. KING follows six days later. (Fact. The Hollywood Reporter India; Wikipedia: Avengers: Doomsday) The trade wants a clash. This book reads a relay. (Analysis)

The class of ’65. Robert Downey Jr. was born on 4 April 1965. Shah Rukh Khan was born on 2 November 1965. (Fact) Same year, two hemispheres, two careers that went from boy wonder to written off to the biggest comebacks in their industries. Both once saved the world in a suit, one red, one blue: Iron Man in 2008, built in a cave from a box of scraps; G.One in 2011, stepping out of a video game his creator built for his son. This Christmas, at 61, both walk to the dark side of the screen. Retirement plans: not found. (Analysis)

The campaigns could not be more different. Marvel has sold its film in public since July 2024, and reported US$50 million in presales by early September 2026. KING had released a title video, a date teaser and almost nothing else by October 2026. (Fact. Wikipedia: Avengers: Doomsday) One campaign spends money to build expectation. The other spends silence, and lets the fans do the building. (Analysis)

The tale of the tape, with respect to both corners: one villain puts on a metal mask; the other does not need one, and raises an eyebrow. One snaps his fingers and half the universe goes quiet; the other opens his arms and the other half comes running. Six days apart is not a clash. It is time enough to watch one, argue about it, and queue for the other. Final score: Cinema 2, Boredom 0. (Analysis)

The other campaign, in numbers. Marvel released the first full Doomsday trailer on 20 July 2026, ahead of its San Diego Comic-Con panel. It drew 503 million views in its first 24 hours: the second-biggest trailer launch on record, behind Sony’s Spider-Man: Brand New Day at 719 million, and ahead of Disney’s previous best, the Deadpool & Wolverine Super Bowl teaser at 365 million. (Fact. Variety India, 22 Jul 2026) Marvel has published no budget. The podcaster John Campea said in April 2026 that he had heard of about US$400 million to make the film and a little over US$300 million to market it. (Fact, as reported. SuperHeroHype, 14 Apr 2026) [Unverified] until Disney or a trade filing publishes a figure.

The screen squeeze. India had 34 IMAX screens in 2025. Dune: Part Three is reported to hold a one-week IMAX exclusive; an unnamed source familiar with IMAX told Variety India that Doomsday would likely take “Experience in IMAX” screens in its second week, adding that nothing was confirmed. (Fact, as reported. Variety India, 24 Mar 2026) The trade read it three ways. Taran Adarsh called KING a much bigger film in India than any Hollywood title. Ramesh Bala expected premium screens to tighten in the opening week, but saw KING’s later date gaining from holiday spillover and repeat viewing. Vishek Chauhan, a distributor and exhibitor, said the clash would take screen space without stopping audiences, citing Barbie and Oppenheimer running side by side. (Fact. Variety India, 20 Mar 2026)

If the IMAX reports hold, Doomsday’s second week is KING’s first: the relay’s hand-off happens on the biggest screens in the country. No premium-format plan for KING had been announced by October 2026. (Analysis; the last sentence Fact, as of Variety India’s 24 Mar 2026 report and this book’s October 2026 check)
