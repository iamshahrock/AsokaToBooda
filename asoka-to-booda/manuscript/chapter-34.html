Chapter 34 · SRKWorld: a fan-franchising operating system

(Proposal) The return of SRKWorld, in the author’s design, is not a website. It is an operating system: shared rules under which independent fan communities create, collaborate and earn around a star’s world, instead of a campaign run entirely by a central marketing team.

SRKWorld.com, 2000: the first version of the idea. (Archive: the author's collection)

  • The film stays the centre: the event, the story and the intellectual property, owned by its owners.
  • The fan brings identity, talent, imagination, community and original work.
  • ijOO supplies a parallel universe of characters, stories and creative assets for fans to build with.
  • The AI Bioskope turns watching into making: storytelling, visual creation and transformations, with the audience as co-creator.
  • O TO X: My Math supplies the rules: original submissions, a shared timed round, transparent scoring (Part IX).
  • Fan communities run the challenges, screenings and events.

(Proposal) What it would measure is not likes. It is original submissions, repeat participation, creative range, and the number of fan assets that earn permission for further use. And it would say plainly that participation is not commercial value: a million fan creations do not make a million commercial properties. (Analysis)

The rights question is the design. Any real fan franchise has to answer, before launch: who owns fan-made work; who controls the official IP; how creators are credited and paid; how AI is used, with what consent for face and voice; how the platform pays for itself; and how fans, especially minors, are protected. (Analysis) This is the difference between a participatory entertainment economy and an uncontrolled extraction of fan labour. (Analysis)
