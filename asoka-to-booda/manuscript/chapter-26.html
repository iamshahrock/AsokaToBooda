His three biggest films: Jawan, Pathaan, Dunki. (Image: Final Thesis archive)

Set the year against what came before it. The five years from 2018 to 2022 produced one lead release, Zero, at about ₹191 crore. The single year 2023 produced about fourteen times as much from three. (Fact, arithmetic from Chapters 15 and 26) The trade site Koimoi reported on 29 December 2023 that he had become the only Indian actor to pass ₹2,500 crore worldwide in a single calendar year, and that no other actor had crossed even ₹1,500 crore in one year. (Fact. Koimoi, 29 Dec 2023; a trade count, not an audited database) He was 57 when the year began. (Fact)

The market followed the box office, not the fame. His celebrity brand value, which Kroll had put at tenth in India for 2022, at the end of the years without a film, more than doubled to US$120.7 million and third place for 2023. (Fact. exchange4media, 21 Mar 2023; afaqs, 19 June 2024) The fans had stayed through the silence. The market came back with the films. (Analysis)

The man everyone had written off had not lost his audience. He had stopped asking for it, and it came anyway. Was this a comeback, or had he simply never left? And if silence can sell a ₹1,000-crore film, what can the showman do when he speaks again? (Analysis; the author’s questions)
