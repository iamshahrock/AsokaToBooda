Chapter 44 · The Rules of the Game

The King’s last move is yours. What if, after reading the story of a King who kept reinventing the game, you got to play one yourself? An independent creative concept by the author; not an official game of Shah Rukh Khan, Red Chillies Entertainment or KING.

(Proposal) Every career in this book moved from zero to something. Zero was the 0 of Zero to One; Aryan Infomatics began with a byte, a 0 and a 1. O TO X takes the idea and hands it to the reader: you start at zero, and X is whatever you make of it.

The King on the board. (Image: Final Thesis archive)

How a round works

  1. Choose your route. Every transformation opens a branch: ZERO → KING, the cinematic universe; ZERO → G.ONE, innovation and technology; ZERO → RA.ONE, rivalry and strategy; ZERO → MY, your own universe.
  2. Bring your own world. Add a tile of your own life, a holiday, a memory, a place, so that every player’s zero is different.
  3. Write your idea in exactly 100 words. One original creation: a character, a scene, a twist, a mission.
  4. Play the shared round. Sixty seconds, the same clock for everyone in the room, in a private room with friends or a public match with strangers.
  5. Get scored in the open. The server, not the player’s browser, checks each submission and awards points by published rules: originality, completion, the challenge result and collaboration. The scores are game points, never money.

(Proposal; the author’s game design)

Where and how it is played. In a browser, on a phone or a desktop, with nothing to install. A player opens a private room with a code for friends, or joins public matchmaking with strangers; everyone in a room shares one 60-second clock. The essential rule is that the browser never decides its own score: it submits the creation, and the server checks it and awards the points. (Proposal; the author’s game design)

For beginners there is a Zero Learning Mode: no coding, no complicated menus, no financial knowledge, only a tile, a choice, a score and a result. Every action moves the player along a personal Byte Journey from 0 to 1 and beyond, unlocking titles, tiles and themes through play and verified creative milestones, not through spending. (Proposal)
