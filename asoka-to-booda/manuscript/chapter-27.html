Chapter 27 · The Brand Universe

After 2023 he did not rush into the next film. For almost three years there was no new release. Instead the institution he had built came back into view, piece by piece: the brands, a city in the desert, a cricket ground in America, a billionaire’s ledger and, quietly, a stake in artificial intelligence. Very few connected the pieces to the boy who built a website in 2000.

From the Hyundai Santro in 1998 to Visa in 2026, he has sold more than films. Hyundai’s former president BVR Subbu has said Khan typed the first agreement himself; by 2025 the partnership had passed 25 years. (Fact. Storyboard18; afaqs, 3 Nov 2025) An actor who reads his own contracts is a businessman before he is an endorser. (Analysis) A 2019 survey of his advertising lists Nokia, Pepsi, Airtel, Lux, Compaq, Dish TV, ICICI, BYJU’S, BigBasket, Visit Dubai and Netflix among more than two dozen others; Thums Up, Castrol, Myntra and Candere followed, and on 3 June 2026 he became Visa’s brand ambassador for India. (Fact. Social Samosa, 2 Nov 2019; afaqs; Visa, 3 June 2026) That is more than 35 brands in about 37 years. (Fact, count from the sources) [Unverified] start and end years for most of them.

A brand launch, one of more than 35 brands in about 37 years. (Image: Final Thesis archive)

The ad that gave his face away. For Diwali 2021, which fell weeks after his son’s arrest, Cadbury’s owner Mondelez, with Ogilvy, Wavemaker and the AI company Rephrase.ai, used machine learning to recreate his face and voice so that small shops hit by Covid could each have their own Shah Rukh Khan advertisement naming their store. It won two Gold, one Silver and one Bronze Lion at Cannes in 2022. (Fact. exchange4media, 25 June 2022) Ogilvy India’s work for Mondelez, entered as “Shah Rukh Khan My Ad”, also won a Titanium Lion. (Fact. BW Marketing World) This may be the most important advertisement in this book. Five years before fans began making him with AI tools, a brand did it with his consent and paid for it: the face was licensed, the use was named and the beneficiaries were small traders. It is the model Part VII argues for. (Analysis)

Pathaan, Jawan, Dunki: the box-office comeback becomes a brand comeback

The scale of the 2023 return is not merely a film-marketing story. It is a multi-sector commercial story.

The three films generated a reported combined worldwide box-office total of more than ₹2,600 crore by January 2024, according to trade and entertainment reporting.

The film releases also coincided with a rapid expansion in his endorsement portfolio. A January 2024 report noted more than ten new brand deals since January 2023 and cited TAM Media Research data showing that Khan appeared in 21 brand campaigns on television between January and July 2023.

(Final Thesis, section 12)

Beyond the film: brands, business and the expanding public profile

The post-resurrection story extends beyond the theatrical business.

Shah Rukh Khan’s public profile includes endorsements, production, cricket franchise ownership, real-estate-related commercial activity, international appearances and investments.

These activities matter because they represent different forms of value around a celebrity.

A film generates theatrical and ancillary revenue. An endorsement connects a celebrity to a brand’s distribution and marketing. A sports franchise creates a relationship with a team, a competition, sponsors and fans. A technology investment creates exposure to a different kind of business.

But these activities must not be collapsed into a single, assumed master strategy.

(Final Thesis, section 63)
