Chapter 33 · The 2000 Playbook, Revisited

Everything in this Part is labelled Proposal or Analysis unless it says otherwise. SRKWorld’s return, ijOO, the AI Bioskope and the fan-franchising operating system are the author’s concepts. None is an announced plan of Shah Rukh Khan, Red Chillies Entertainment, the makers of KING or any company. Rumour has it something is cooking. This Part is about what could be cooked.

Take a leaf out of his own book. In 2000 he tried to build a direct, permanent line to his audience, with his own name on the address, and it failed because India had no broadband, no smartphones and no habit of looking at a small screen for a star. (Fact; Analysis, Part II) In 2026 all three exist in the hands of hundreds of millions of people, and so does the technology to let them make things, not just watch them. (Analysis)

SRKWorld.com, 2000: The Galleria. The 2000 playbook. (Archive: the author's collection)
