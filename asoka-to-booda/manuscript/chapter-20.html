Now the recollection, kept apart. On 4 October 2021, the day after the arrest, the author saw no vigil outside Mannat: not a single fan standing in silence at the gate of the most celebrated house in India. (Recollection) Every year on 2 November crowds fill that same road for one wave from the balcony. In her account, that day the road was quiet. (Recollection) Later reports describe fans gathering outside the house in the weeks that followed, and police clearing crowds; they do not settle what the street looked like on 4 October, and her account is offered as testimony, not as a headcount. (Fact; Method note)

This book does not argue the case. It asks what the case revealed. What does it say about due process when a young man spends more than three weeks in custody in a case that later finds no evidence against him? What does mass fandom mean when the person at its centre is in distress? (Analysis; the author’s questions)
