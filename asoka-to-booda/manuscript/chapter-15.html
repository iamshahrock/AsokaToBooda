Chapter 15 · The Box-Office Graph

The career-long view, as drawn in the Final Thesis: principal theatrical films, worldwide gross, Bollywood Hungama estimates. (Chart: Final Thesis)

The curve tells the story the matrix implies. From 2010 to 2013 each film was bigger than the last, up to Chennai Express. From 2014 to 2018 the seven films never reached that level again, and the line falls to its lowest point with Zero. Then there is a gap of four years with no lead release at all, and in 2023 the line goes through the roof: two films past ₹1,000 crore and a third past ₹400 crore. (Analysis)

Two cautions keep the graph honest. Gross is ticket sales, not profit; a film can gross less and still earn more for its producer through satellite, streaming and music rights, which are not published film by film. And the money and the innovation are different measures, drawn on different charts in this book for a reason: a high gross does not prove a good campaign, and a weak gross does not prove a bad one. Zero had the most inventive campaign of the five years and the weakest result. (Method note; Analysis)
