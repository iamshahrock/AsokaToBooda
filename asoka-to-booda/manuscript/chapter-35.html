Chapter 35 · ijOO and the AI Bioskope

(Proposal) ijOO is the author’s original universe: its own characters and mythology, built for participatory storytelling, in which fans can create without borrowing anyone’s face. Its art is the author’s own. The AI Bioskope is the proposed stage for it: a cinema that the audience makes, where a fan’s idea becomes a scene, a scene becomes a story, and the story is credited to the fan who made it.
