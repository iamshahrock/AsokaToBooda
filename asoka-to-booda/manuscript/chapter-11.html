A franchise needs more than a launch. It needs continuity, rights management, investment and, above all, a reason for the audience to keep playing after the credits. Ra.One delivered the launch. The universe never got its second chapter, and SRKWorld, in the form the author says they dreamed of, never came back. (Analysis; Recollection)

2012: a cup, a ban and a farewell. Between the superhero and the train came a year that showed every side of the institution. In May 2012, after the Knight Riders beat Mumbai Indians at the Wankhede, he argued with officials and guards; the Mumbai Cricket Association banned him from the stadium for five years. He said the guards had misbehaved with children, his daughter among them. Eleven days later, on 27 May, the same team won its first IPL title, in Chennai. (Fact. Daily FT, 19 May 2012; ESPNcricinfo) The ban was lifted in August 2015. (Fact. Deccan Chronicle, 2 Aug 2015) On 12 April 2012 he gave Yale’s Chubb Fellowship lecture. (Fact. Yale News) On 21 October Yash Chopra died, before Jab Tak Hai Jaan opened; it grossed about ₹236 crore worldwide. The director of Darr and Dil To Pagal Hai closed his career with him. (Fact. Wikipedia: Jab Tak Hai Jaan; Analysis)

He had won a cup in Chennai and lost a stadium in Mumbai in the same month. His next film would take a train towards the south, and this time the audience would drive. (Analysis)

The richest year without a new universe. The money did not wait for a sequel. In January 2013 Forbes India published its first Celebrity 100 list, ranked by income and popularity, and put him first: about ₹202.8 crore earned between October 2011 and September 2012, the window that opened with Ra.One. Salman Khan was second with about ₹144.2 crore and M.S. Dhoni third with about ₹135.16 crore. (Fact. Koimoi, 24 Jan 2013) The figure is a Forbes India estimate, not a published account. (Method note)

The Diwali screens fight. Jab Tak Hai Jaan opened on 13 November 2012, on Diwali, against Ajay Devgn’s Son of Sardaar. (Fact. Bollywood Hungama, 15 Nov 2013) Ajay Devgn Films complained to the Competition Commission of India that Yash Raj Films had tied exhibitors: Ek Tha Tiger only if they also took Jab Tak Hai Jaan for Diwali. The same report said his previous film had opened on 1,200 screens and he was struggling to secure 400. (Fact. Sunday Times, 11 Nov 2012) The Commission found no antitrust violation, and on 13 November 2013 the Competition Appellate Tribunal dismissed the appeal. (Fact. PYMNTS/CPI, 6 Nov 2012; Bollywood Hungama, 15 Nov 2013) The lesson for a fan franchise is plain: in 2012 the screens, not the fans, still decided how many people could see a film on its first weekend. (Analysis)

The part of the empire that did survive. What outlived Ra.One was the workshop behind it. Red Chillies VFX, founded in 2006, went on to lead the effects of another studio’s superhero, Rakesh Roshan’s Krrish 3 (2013), with Pixion also contributing; Cartoon Brew put the film’s effects cost at $3.9 million. (Fact. Cartoon Brew, 15 Jan 2017; VFX Voice, 2 Oct 2017) VFX Voice names Ra.One and Krrish 3 as the films that first won the house recognition. (Fact. VFX Voice, 2 Oct 2017) The universe had no second chapter, but the skill built for it became a business that sold to the rest of the industry. (Analysis)

The small screen, three months later. Ra.One reached television on 21 January 2012, on Star Gold, three months after its cinema release. (Fact. exchange4media, 23 Jan 2012) Its premiere rated 6.7, below the channel’s own premieres of Bodyguard (10) and Singham (8.7). (Fact. exchange4media, 28 Feb 2013) A forum copy of an Indiantelevision report gives the same 6.7 with a 28 per cent market share, a 9 pm slot, and Dabangg (9.2, Colors) and 3 Idiots (10.88, Set) as the records it did not break. (Fact. DreamDTH, citing Indiantelevision, 2012) The first report calls the figure a TRP, the second a TVR; the number is the same. (Method note)

The superhero next door got its sequels. The film whose effects Red Chillies VFX led belonged to a family that kept going. Rakesh Roshan’s series began with Koi… Mil Gaya (2003), continued with Krrish (2006) and Krrish 3 (2013). (Fact. ThePrint/ANI, 28 Mar 2025) The Numbers counts Krrish 3 at $43,337,264 worldwide, $41,213,931 of it outside the United States. (Fact. The Numbers) On 28 March 2025 Rakesh Roshan announced Krrish 4, with Hrithik Roshan directing and starring, produced by Yash Raj Films with him. (Fact. ThePrint/ANI, 28 Mar 2025)

The two superheroes were built by the same effects house within two years of each other. One stayed a single film; the other became four, owned by a family that treated it as a series from the start. The difference was not technology. It was the decision to keep making chapters. (Analysis)
