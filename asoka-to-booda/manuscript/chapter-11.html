A franchise needs more than a launch. It needs continuity, rights management, investment and, above all, a reason for the audience to keep playing after the credits. Ra.One delivered the launch. The universe never got its second chapter, and SRKWorld, in the form the author says they dreamed of, never came back. (Analysis; Recollection)

2012: a cup, a ban and a farewell. Between the superhero and the train came a year that showed every side of the institution. In May 2012, after the Knight Riders beat Mumbai Indians at the Wankhede, he argued with officials and guards; the Mumbai Cricket Association banned him from the stadium for five years. He said the guards had misbehaved with children, his daughter among them. Eleven days later, on 27 May, the same team won its first IPL title, in Chennai. (Fact. Daily FT, 19 May 2012; ESPNcricinfo) The ban was lifted in August 2015. (Fact. Deccan Chronicle, 2 Aug 2015) On 12 April 2012 he gave Yale’s Chubb Fellowship lecture. (Fact. Yale News) On 21 October Yash Chopra died, before Jab Tak Hai Jaan opened; it grossed about ₹236 crore worldwide. The director of Darr and Dil To Pagal Hai closed his career with him. (Fact. Wikipedia: Jab Tak Hai Jaan; Analysis)

He had won a cup in Chennai and lost a stadium in Mumbai in the same month. His next film would take a train towards the south, and this time the audience would drive. (Analysis)
