Chapter 24 · Jawan: the fire

The second film of the year was his own. Jawan, Atlee’s Hindi debut, produced by Gauri Khan for Red Chillies on a reported budget of ₹300 crore, opened on 7 September 2023 with ₹75.4 crore net in India, a record for a Hindi film. Its pre-release event was held in Chennai on 30 August, at a college, not a press hall. (Fact. Wikipedia: Jawan) It grossed ₹1,148.32 crore worldwide, the highest-grossing Indian film of 2023 and the highest-grossing Hindi film in India. (Fact. same source, citing Bollywood Hungama; Madhyamam counted ₹1,138 crore by week six)

Jawan (2023)

Two years later it won him his first National Film Award for Best Actor, announced on 1 August 2025 and presented by President Droupadi Murmu on 23 September 2025, shared with Vikrant Massey, thirty-three years after Deewana. (Fact. The Statesman) His message to his fans after the announcement, recorded with an injured arm: “just keep the popcorn ready.” (Fact. IANS, 1 Aug 2025)

A southern engine. The film was built to travel south as much as north. Atlee came to it from the Tamil hits Mersal (2017) and Bigil (2019); Nayanthara, the “lady superstar” of South Indian cinema, made her Hindi debut opposite him; Vijay Sethupathi played the arms dealer Kalee; Deepika Padukone came in for a cameo. (Fact. The National, 5 Sept 2023) Two days before release, The National listed it opening in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada; when it reached Netflix, it streamed in Hindi, Tamil and Telugu. (Fact. same source; The Week, 2 Nov 2023)

Jawan: first day, first show, September 2023. (Images: Final Thesis archive)

The music went first. Three songs were released ahead of the film: “Zinda Banda” on 31 July, “Chaleya” on 14 August and “Not Ramaiya Vastavaiya” on 29 August 2023. By 5 September they had passed 64 million, 57 million and 33 million YouTube views. T-Series reportedly paid ₹32 crore (320 million rupees) for the music rights, a record for a Hindi album. (Fact. The National, 5 Sept 2023)

A trailer on a tower, a cut on his birthday. On 28 August 2023 he announced that the trailer would launch at Dubai’s Burj Khalifa on 31 August at 9 pm, where he would meet fans, and he asked them to come dressed in red. (Fact. Khaleej Times, 28 Aug 2023) On 2 November 2023, his 58th birthday, Netflix released an extended cut at midnight. (Fact. The Week, 2 Nov 2023) It became the most-watched film in India on Netflix, across all languages, in its first two weeks. (Fact. Film Companion)

Read together, the dates make a calendar used as a stage: songs a month out, a college in Chennai and a tower in Dubai in the last week, and the streaming release placed on the day his fans already gather outside Mannat. It is the Chennai Express lesson run at the scale of a home production: the audience is given events to attend, not only a film to buy. (Analysis)
