A · Method

The creative story ends with the game. The research record follows it: how this book was made, the data it rests on, what it corrected, what it still cannot prove, and where every fact came from.

What this book is made of. This manuscript integrates every version of the thesis into one: the 384-page ASOKA_TO_BOODA_FINAL_THESIS document; the three full drafts, The Silence Before the King (long form), Asoka to Booda · Be Da Boo-Da with its revised “All In” chapter and the Royal Flush, and From Zero to King (official first draft); the case study The Silence Before the King, pages 1 to 19; the earlier web edition, SRK: The Man, The Myth, The King; the essay Asoka to Boo-Da; the Chennai Express case study of 2013; and the author’s archive of SRKWorld screens, press photographs and video. Each fact is told once, in its best-sourced version, in the chapter where it happens. The originals are kept, unchanged, at agarmainkinghota.com. (Method note)

Four voices. Every statement carries one label. Fact: the public record, with its source. Recollection: the author’s own testimony, as co-founder of SRKWorld and digital strategist on Ra.One and Chennai Express. Analysis: argument from the evidence, open to disagreement. Proposal: something that could be built; never an announced plan of Shah Rukh Khan or any company. (Method note)

Sources, in order of weight. Primary documents first: court and agency records, company and government releases, the Chennai Express case study, the author’s archive. Then the trade and news press, named and dated. Film data, budgets and grosses come from each film’s Wikipedia page as of October 2026, which cites the trade sources; where a page or two trackers disagree, both figures or the range is printed. (Method note)

Numbers. A gross is ticket sales, not profit. Different measures are never merged into one series: worldwide gross and India net, TRAI subscriptions and World Bank users, posts and people. A number that could not be found is written N/A. Ratios and totals are this book’s arithmetic and are labelled so. (Method note)

What this book leaves out on purpose. The private life of the family beyond what it made public; the private turmoil of October 2021, which no one has described and this book does not imagine; the contact details of anyone named; and private business documents shared with the author in confidence. (Method note)
