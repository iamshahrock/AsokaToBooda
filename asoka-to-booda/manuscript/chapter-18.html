Chapter 18 · Five Conclusions

  1. Ra.One set the franchise ambition. Its significance was the attempt to make a film character live beyond the film, through games, merchandise, technology and a relationship with the audience.
  2. Chennai Express set the benchmark for an integrated campaign. Its innovation was the combination: participation, original digital products, coordinated fan clubs, analytics, brands and creative decisions changed every day.
  3. Later campaigns kept individual innovations but not the architecture. Happy New Year, Dear Zindagi, Jab Harry Met Sejal and Zero all experimented. Their innovations were tied to one film, one campaign or one platform.
  4. Zero is the most important qualification. Bauua Ki Toli, the character’s own accounts, the Alexa skill and the Snapchat lens show real digital ambition in 2018. Any claim of a complete withdrawal from innovation has to answer for them.
  5. The gap is in sustained, technology-led franchise building. The evidence shows a less consistent continuation of the integrated model. It does not, on its own, explain why, or who decided.

(Analysis, on the evidence of Chapters 10 to 17)

In one sentence: between Ra.One and Chennai Express, his film marketing joined technology, fan participation, original entertainment products, merchandise and data into one system; from 2014 to Zero in 2018, single campaigns kept inventing, but the system was not rebuilt; his personal involvement is documented in the first period, and the reasons for the change in the second are not yet on the record. (Analysis)

Zero opened on 21 December 2018. Four months later, in Beijing, where it closed the Beijing International Film Festival, he told CGTN: “Maybe I made the wrong film.” If something does not excite you in the morning, he said, you should not wake up. (Fact. Gulf News / IANS, 19 Apr 2019) Then he did something no one expected. He stopped. His next lead release would be more than four years away. (Fact)

In the language of this book, Zero is the 0 in Zero to One: the lowest point, and the start of the climb. And then, nothing.
