Chapter 3 · Campus Cackle: the rehearsal room, 1985 to 1988

[image: SRKWorld.com, 2000: the theatre page]

SRKWorld.com, 2000: the page on his struggle and early theatre. (Archive: the author’s collection)

Officially, from 1985 to 1988, he studied economics at Hansraj College. In practice he lived in a rehearsal room. (Fact)

The room belonged to the Theatre Action Group, founded in Delhi in 1973 by Barry John, a British director who had arrived in India in 1968 and stayed. John staged more than a hundred productions in Hindi and English; the actors who trained with him include Raghuvir Yadav, Manoj Bajpayee and Shah Rukh Khan. (Fact. Outlook, 6 Feb 2022) Money was so short, Bajpayee remembers, that the young actors passed one cigarette around rather than smoke one each. Khan stayed only briefly before leaving for Mumbai; Bajpayee stayed on until Bandit Queen. (Fact. Manoj Bajpayee’s account, Bollywood Hungama, 17 Dec 2024) One poor room in Delhi produced two of the most admired screen actors of the next thirty years, by opposite routes: one left fast for the mass audience, the other stayed for the craft and reached the mass audience later. (Analysis)

Asked about his famous student around Teacher’s Day 2013, John said the young Khan “was destined to be a somebody”, and listed why: talent, perseverance, self-belief, a refusal to give up, and some luck. Then he added: “Nobody can make anybody.” (Fact. DNA, September 2013, as reproduced by India Forums, 6 Sept 2013) [Unverified] the original DNA article’s date and link.

After graduating he cleared the IIT entrance examination, because his mother insisted. [Unverified] the year and the exam’s name. He joined the master’s programme in mass communication at Jamia Millia Islamia and could not sit the final examination because his attendance was short; the director Kabir Khan, his junior, studied from his notes. (Fact. The Tribune, 22 Feb 2019) In 2018 Jamia asked the education ministry for permission to give him an honorary doctorate. The ministry declined, reportedly because he already held one, from the Maulana Azad National Urdu University in 2016. (Fact. same source)

The subject he left unfinished became the work of his life. Twelve years later he would put his own life online, on a website he co-founded. The classroom taught him the theory of mass communication; Delhi’s theatres taught him the audience. (Analysis)

Delhi in the mid-1980s gave a young actor three classrooms that charged no fees: the National School of Drama, where he had watched rehearsals as a boy; Barry John’s group, where he learned to work a stage; and Doordarshan, which was about to need new faces. He used all three before he was twenty-four. None gave him a certificate. (Analysis)

The campus remembered him as more than an actor in waiting. Anurag Kashyap, who followed him at Hansraj, has said Khan captained the college’s hockey and basketball teams, was its sportsman of the year and topped his class in economics. (Fact. Anurag Kashyap’s account to BookMyShow, WION, 2 Nov 2025) [Unverified] the captaincies, the sports award and the class rank rest on one fellow student’s memory; Hansraj College’s records would settle them. When Deewana opened in 1992, Kashyap recalls, the students booked out Delhi’s Amba cinema, and his entry scene drew so much noise that nobody heard the song. (Fact. same source) The college had become his first audience before the country did. (Analysis)

The degree itself waited twenty-eight years. In February 2016, in Delhi to launch the anthem of Fan, he went back to Hansraj and received his bachelor’s degree in economics from the principal, Rama Sharma, who said the college had kept it safe all that time; he signed the register. (Fact. PTI, Morung Express, 17 Feb 2016) IANS reported thousands of fans outside, and Fan’s director Maneesh Sharma, also a Hansraj alumnus, beside him. (Fact. IANS, Gulf News, updated 15 Sept 2018) His line that day: “I am back in my college which I left in 1988.” The University of Delhi’s alumni archive, which reproduces it, also records that classmates helped him by making carbon copies of their notes, and that he named Barry John of TAG, then in Jangpura Extension, as his acting mentor. (Fact. University of Delhi alumni archive) The sources disagree on the day: PTI dates it 17 February 2016, IANS calls it a Tuesday (16 February 2016), and the archive files the clipping under 16.12.2015. [Unverified] the exact date; the college’s register would settle it. (Method note)

The IIT story above also has a second version. In a 2000 BBC interview, as Siasat retold it in 2025, the test came before college, not after: he had studied science at school and wanted economics, and his mother first asked him to pass the IIT exam; he passed, and she let him switch. (Fact. Siasat, 6 Sept 2025) Neither account names the year or the exam. [Unverified] whether the test came before or after Hansraj; the BBC’s original 2000 interview would settle it. (Method note) Either way, the pattern holds: he met a parent’s condition first, then took the path he had chosen. (Analysis)
