13.3 The RA.ONE–Chennai Express benchmark

RA.ONE · 2011

The entertainment-franchise ambition

Character-led intellectual property, gaming, merchandise, technology partnerships and the attempt to extend a cinematic property beyond the screen.

CHENNAI EXPRESS · 2013

The integrated participation model

Social campaign mechanics, fan coordination, original digital products, interactive experiences, brand associations and campaign analytics.

Together, the two case studies provide the opening benchmark for the rest of the investigation.

RA.ONE asks how a film can become the foundation of a wider entertainment property. Chennai Express asks how digital campaign architecture can make audiences active participants in the promotional experience.

The relationship between those two ideas—an expandable fictional world and a participatory audience—is central to the later proposal for KING.

Part II conclusion

The evidence presented in this part supports a specific historical proposition: by 2013, the Shah Rukh Khan campaign ecosystem described in the supplied case-study material incorporated multiple forms of digital participation, original interactive products, fan coordination and campaign measurement.

The next part examines what changed after Chennai Express, including the apparent lull, the changing forms of digital innovation, and the importance of ZERO as a counterexample to any claim that experimentation simply stopped.
