Original Digital Products, Games, Apps and Interactive Experiences

10.1 A campaign that offered entertainment, not only information

The Chennai Express campaign's digital products are important because they expanded the audience's options for engagement.

The original case-study material identifies browser games, mobile gaming, interactive video and a karaoke app. Each offered a different form of participation and a different relationship to the film.

Digital experience Documented mechanic Marketing significance
Browser games Seven to eight games adapted for websites, mobile and social platforms. Multiple entry points into the campaign across digital environments.
Escape from Rameshwaram An endless-running game featuring Rahul and Meena, with obstacles, unlockable characters and upgrades. Translated elements of the film into an interactive entertainment format.
Interactive video Users could influence what Rahul did next and change the sequence of a romantic story. Gave audiences a degree of agency over the viewing experience.
Karaoke app Fans could sing film songs, share scores and dedicate songs to friends. Connected music, social sharing and audience-created participation.

Source: the supplied Chennai Express case-study material, particularly pages 5–14, as referenced in the thesis source.

10.2 The strategic difference between formats

These products did not all perform the same function.

The browser games and mobile game created repeatable entertainment activities. The interactive video introduced choice into the viewing experience. The karaoke app invited fans to perform, score and share.

Together, these formats illustrate a broader campaign principle: participation need not take one form.

An effective participatory campaign can offer different levels of engagement, from a simple social action to a more involved digital experience.

This is particularly relevant to the later KING proposition, where participation could potentially include storytelling, creative submissions, games and fan-made content. Such a future proposal must, however, be distinguished from the documented historical campaign.

10.3 The significance of original digital products

The central research point is that the Chennai Express campaign did not limit its digital activity to publishing film-related content across online channels.

It developed specific products and mechanics through which audiences could interact with the campaign.

That distinction is fundamental to the comparison between digital promotion and digital product innovation.
