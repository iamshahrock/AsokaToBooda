5. The personal involvement question: what can the evidence establish?

Your central claim is that Khan's personal passion for technology-led marketing was a major creative engine during the RA.ONE and Chennai Express period, and that the later campaigns became more conventional as that personal involvement diminished.

There are three different evidentiary questions here.

1. Did Khan personally support and participate in the earlier technology-led approach?

Documented

Yes, at the level of public statements and campaign documentation. The Chennai Express case study attributes visionary ideas to him, records his statements about technology and audience connection, and documents his involvement with the game and karaoke experiences.

ce_digitalcasestudy.pdf

2. Did the nature of the publicly visible marketing change after 2013?

Supported, with exceptions

The comparison indicates a shift in emphasis toward promotional content, platform-native activations, and release-specific engagement. Happy New Year, Dear Zindagi, Jab Harry Met Sejal, and ZERO nevertheless demonstrate continued innovation, so the evidence supports a change in pattern, not a complete cessation.

3. Did Khan become personally disinterested in technology marketing, or did employees simply carry on without his creative direction?

Requires internal evidence

Public campaigns alone cannot establish this. A conventional-looking campaign could reflect a deliberate strategy, production constraints, the director's preferences, rights and licensing limits, a studio decision, the available technology, or a different allocation of Khan's time. None of those explanations should be assumed to be the cause.

A professional case study should therefore treat the final point as a hypothesis to be investigated—not as a conclusion already proven by the output.

Your firsthand experience and campaign records may provide evidence that public reporting cannot. Internal briefs, pitch decks, creative approvals, campaign correspondence, development records, and interviews with the teams could help distinguish Khan's own creative involvement from the work of the marketing department, agency, producer, or platform partner.

That distinction is important because a celebrity's participation in a campaign is not the same as personally originating its core technology or approving every execution.

  1. Professional findings

Comparative conclusions from the documented 2011–2018 campaigns

Five conclusions

01

RA.ONE established the franchise ambition.

Its significance was the attempt to make a film character commercially and digitally extensible through games, merchandise, technology, and audience relationships.

02

Chennai Express established the integrated campaign benchmark.

The defining innovation was the combination of participation, original digital products, fan-club coordination, analytics, brand integration, and real-time creative adaptation.

03

Later campaigns retained individual innovations but did not consistently reproduce the same architecture.

Happy New Year, Dear Zindagi, Jab Harry Met Sejal, and ZERO show genuine experimentation. Their documented innovations, however, are more often specific to a film, campaign, or platform.

04

ZERO is the most important qualification to the thesis.

Bauua Ki Toli, character-led social media, Alexa, and Snapchat AR show that the 2018 campaign still had substantial digital ambition. Any claim of a complete or permanent withdrawal from innovation would need to account for this.

05

The gap is clearest in sustained technology-led franchise building.

The evidence points to a less consistent continuation of the integrated model pioneered in the earlier campaigns. It does not, by itself, explain why that shift occurred or who was responsible for it.

Final assessment

The strongest professional formulation of your thesis would be:

Between RA.ONE and Chennai Express, Shah Rukh Khan's film marketing demonstrated an unusually integrated approach to digital innovation, combining technology, fan participation, original entertainment products, merchandising, data-driven strategy, and the creation of continuing character-based experiences. From 2014 through ZERO in 2018, individual campaigns continued to introduce creative digital innovations, but the publicly documented record suggests a shift toward more campaign-specific, content-led, and platform-led activity rather than consistent continuation of that integrated model. Khan's personal involvement was explicitly documented in the earlier campaigns; whether his later reduced visibility reflected personal disinterest, organisational decisions, or other circumstances requires additional evidence.

That formulation preserves the force of your observation while making it credible to a professional audience.

My recommendation: use RA.ONE, G.ONE merchandising, and Chennai Express as the primary case studies; use Happy New Year, Fan, Dear Zindagi, Jab Harry Met Sejal, and ZERO as the exceptions and transition cases; and use the other films to establish the broader campaign pattern.

This would make the study more persuasive than a simple ranking of which movie had the most innovative marketing. It would establish what was built, what changed, which ideas survived, and what evidence is still needed to understand the shift in creative leadership.
