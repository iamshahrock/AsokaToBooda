Filmography coverage note

The publication master should include a separate, fully reconciled appendix for every verified film credit, with role type, release year, and source-supported gross data where available. The principal-film graph in this draft is a consistent later-period comparison series, not a complete numerical dataset for every screen appearance since 1992.

The full filmography must include supporting roles, guest appearances, cameos and voice performances, but those categories should not be mixed indiscriminately into a graph of lead theatrical releases.

The 2026 release record must also be refreshed before publication. A film scheduled for release after the publication date cannot be assigned a completed worldwide gross.
