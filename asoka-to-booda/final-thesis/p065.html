This is a proposed architecture. It is not evidence that Shah Rukh Khan, Red Chillies Entertainment or the producers of KING have authorised SRKWORLD, ijOO or a fan-franchising arrangement.

That distinction is essential to the credibility of the study.

Why O TO X MY MATH belongs here

The O TO X MY MATH concept provides a more concrete example of the participation mechanics proposed for such an ecosystem.

The game framework includes an original creative submission with a 100-word limit, a selected transformation route, a shared timed round, scoring rules and the possibility of private rooms or public matchmaking.

The underlying principle is simple:

A participant contributes something original. The contribution is transformed under a common framework. The result can be evaluated through transparent rules.

That differs from a conventional promotional contest in which the primary activity is to like, share or comment on a branded post.

The proposed system could measure original submissions, repeat participation, community activity, creative diversity and the number of assets that receive permission for further use.

But participation metrics should not be confused with commercial value.

A million fan-created assets do not automatically create a million commercially viable properties. The rights, quality, consent, moderation, attribution and economics of each use matter.

The rights question

Any genuine fan-franchising model would require clear rules around:

  • The distinction between official intellectual property and > fan-created work.

  • Permission to use names, images, characters, film footage and music.

  • Ownership and licensing of original fan contributions.

  • Compensation and attribution where creative work is commercialised.

  • AI training, face likeness, voice likeness and consent.

  • Moderation, safety, data protection and dispute resolution.

This is not a footnote. It is the difference between a participatory entertainment ecosystem and an uncontrolled extraction of fan labour.

A sustainable model must create value for creators as well as rights holders.

The original proposition of SRKWORLD is therefore not simply to turn fans into marketers.

It is to explore whether fans can become recognised participants in an entertainment economy, with transparent rights and rules.

That is the contribution this case study can make beyond one celebrity's career.
