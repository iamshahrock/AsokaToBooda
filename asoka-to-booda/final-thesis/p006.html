Other releases and appearances within the period

For completeness, the following are not included as full campaign comparisons because Khan was not the principal actor or the available public evidence does not establish a standalone Khan-led campaign:

  • 2011: Always Kabhi Kabhi (producer), Love Breakups Zindagi and > Shahrukh Bola Khoobsurat Hai Tu (special appearances).

  • 2012: Student of the Year (narration/special appearance).

  • 2016: Ae Dil Hai Mushkil (special appearance).

  • 2017: Tubelight (special appearance).

The important methodological distinction is between a film that has online promotion and a film whose campaign was designed around a new digital mechanism. The table is intended to assess the latter, not to suggest that ordinary publicity did not take place.
