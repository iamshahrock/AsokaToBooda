CHAPTER 4 — ZERO: THE PAUSE BEFORE THE PIVOT

5

When the King became ZERO

In 2018, ZERO brought Shah Rukh Khan into a distinctive cinematic and promotional world built around Bauua Singh.

The campaign used character-led content, WhatsApp stickers, Bauua Ki Toli rewards, Alexa and Snapchat AR. The promotional strategy continued to experiment with the ways a film character could travel across digital platforms.

This is why ZERO matters to the thesis.

It was not a complete withdrawal from digital marketing.

It was a film that continued to explore digital engagement, but did not publicly establish a permanent, interconnected fan ecosystem on the scale of the ambitions associated with Ra.One and Chennai Express.

And after ZERO, the theatrical pause began.

The title has since acquired an almost irresistible symbolic quality.

ZERO is a number, a film title and, in the narrative developed here, a point of departure.

A star who had helped build digital worlds appeared to enter a period of reduced cinematic visibility.

Was it a retreat? A recalibration? A private reassessment? A consequence of the economics and organisational realities of the industry?

The public record cannot tell us.

But it can establish the contrast between the earlier campaign ambitions and the long gap before the next major theatrical phase.

That contrast is the reason ZERO belongs at the centre of this thesis.

Not as proof of failure.

As the end of a chapter whose unfinished business remained unresolved.
