The Silence Before the King

PART IV — THE RESURRECTION

2022–2023: From public absence to a new cinematic force

2022 — The return begins before the films arrive

By 2022, the anticipation surrounding Shah Rukh Khan's return to theatrical cinema had become a story in itself.

The public did not merely await another release. It awaited the return of a star whose screen absence had become part of his public narrative.

The announcement and promotional activity around Pathaan brought that anticipation into focus.

The film's teaser was released in November 2022, ahead of its January 2023 theatrical release. The campaign was notable for its controlled publicity, reliance on trailers and songs, and the use of Khan's social media presence rather than a conventional schedule of extensive media interviews. Contemporary reporting described the campaign as a departure from traditional promotional routines.

The result was a campaign in which absence itself became part of the audience's anticipation.

That does not mean the film's success was caused by silence, nor that the silence was necessarily a deliberate strategy conceived in 2021.

It does mean that the return to cinema was experienced against a backdrop of intense public attention.

2023 — Pathaan: The return becomes a mass event

Pathaan arrived in January 2023 and became a major commercial success.

Its campaign relied on the film's action spectacle, music, trailers, carefully managed publicity and Khan's enormous social-media reach.

The promotional strategy was notable for its restraint in conventional media appearances and its use of direct-to-fan communication. The campaign's public materials and contemporary analysis document the scale of anticipation surrounding the release.

For this thesis, Pathaan represents the first major chapter of the post-2021 cinematic return.

It is tempting to interpret the film as revenge, a response to institutional pressure or a personal message to those who had criticised Khan.

But those are interpretations, not established facts about the actor's motives.

A more supportable reading is that Pathaan demonstrated the continuing commercial and cultural power of Shah Rukh Khan after a period of public absence and extraordinary scrutiny.

The audience returned to the cinema.

The star returned to the screen.

And the public conversation changed.

Jawan — The star becomes a pan-Indian phenomenon

Jawan extended the momentum through a campaign built around multiple looks, action-oriented imagery, music, a preview, trailers and major promotional events.

Its pan-Indian casting and language strategy helped position it for audiences across regions.

The campaign also used a mix of large public events and direct communication with fans, while continuing the relatively limited conventional interview format associated with Khan's recent promotions.

The important feature was the scale of the cinematic event.

Jawan was not simply a follow-up to Pathaan. It reinforced the idea that Khan could occupy the centre of a nationwide entertainment conversation across multiple markets.

It also revived the question of how a star with a global audience might connect that audience to a continuing digital experience beyond the film's theatrical run.

Dunki — A different kind of storytelling

With Dunki, the promotional language changed again.

The campaign used a series of content releases, including the “Drops” format, to introduce elements of the film's story and characters. The first drop was released on Khan's birthday, 2 November 2023, followed by additional promotional material, music and the trailer.

The film offered a contrast to the action spectacle of Pathaan and Jawan.

It returned to a more emotionally grounded narrative and demonstrated the range of Khan's appeal across genres.

Together, the three films established a new phase in his public career.

The 2023 releases were reported to have generated more than ₹2,600 crore in worldwide box-office collections, though such aggregate figures depend on reporting dates and industry estimates. Contemporary industry reporting also documented a substantial expansion in his brand endorsement activity during the year.

This was not simply a comeback in the narrow sense of a successful film release.

It was a restoration of commercial scale, public visibility and cultural centrality.

But the larger question remains: what happens when a cinematic resurrection is followed by a global business expansion—and when the next film is called KING?
