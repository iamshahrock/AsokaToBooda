CHAPTER 7 — 2023: PATHAAN, JAWAN, DUNKI AND THE GREAT RESURRECTION

The King returns—but what exactly has returned?

The year 2023 changed the scale of the conversation.

Pathaan arrived in January. Jawan followed in September. Dunki closed the year in December.

Together, the films demonstrated that Shah Rukh Khan could return to theatrical cinema at extraordinary commercial scale after the long gap following ZERO.

The campaigns were not identical.

Pathaan relied on anticipation, controlled publicity, songs, trailers and direct-to-fan communication. Contemporary reporting highlighted the limited use of conventional media interviews and the campaign's departure from familiar promotional routines.

Jawan used multiple looks, action imagery, a major preview, music, trailers and large promotional events to create a pan-Indian entertainment event.

Dunki used its “Drops” format to release content progressively, introducing characters and story elements through a sequence of promotional releases.

Three films. Three different marketing identities. One star at the centre of an extraordinary year.

Industry reporting placed the combined worldwide box-office collections of the three films above ₹2,600 crore, subject to the reporting dates and methodologies used. Brand endorsement activity also expanded during this period.

The scale of the comeback is not in question.

But the digital-marketing thesis requires a more specific question.

Did the resurrection bring back the digital innovator—or primarily the cinematic superstar?

The distinction matters.

The 2023 campaigns demonstrated the power of anticipation, character, content, platform distribution and a highly engaged audience.

They also demonstrated the continuing effectiveness of a film-specific campaign.

But the public record does not establish that the integrated fan-powered entertainment ecosystem envisaged by Ra.One and Chennai Express had returned as a permanent platform.

The King had returned to the screen.

Whether the digital kingdom had returned is another question.

And that is what makes the transition to KING so intriguing.
