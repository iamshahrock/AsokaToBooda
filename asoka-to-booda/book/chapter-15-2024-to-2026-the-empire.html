Chapter 15

2024 to 2026: the empire

974 words · about 4 min read

After 2023 he did not rush into the next film. For almost three years there was no new release. Instead, the institution he had built in Part One came back into view, piece by piece, and the world started asking to see him. Interpretation

2024: a title and a career award. On 26 May 2024 his Kolkata Knight Riders won their third IPL title. (Fact. Sky Sports, 26 May 2024) On 10 August the Locarno Film Festival gave him its Pardo alla Carriera, a career award. (Fact. bluewin / Keystone-SDA, 2 July 2024) The Hurun India Rich List took him in for the first time in August 2024, and its 2025 list named him a billionaire, a fortune driven by Red Chillies (table below). (Fact. Outlook Business) The company Gauri Khan and he had set up in 2002 had become the asset. Interpretation

The final in Chennai. On 26 May 2024 at the M.A. Chidambaram Stadium, Kolkata bowled Sunrisers Hyderabad out for 114, the lowest total in an IPL final, and won by eight wickets in 10.3 overs, with Shreyas Iyer as captain and Gautam Gambhir as mentor. It was the club's third title and its first in ten years. (Fact. The Telegraph t2, 26 May 2024) The cricket business he bought in 2008 for US$75.09 million (Room · ArclightZ) won its third title the year after his films won back the box office. Interpretation

The same institutions once shut a door on him. In 2012 the Mumbai Cricket Association banned him from the Wankhede Stadium for five years, and lifted the ban in 2015. (Fact. Deccan Chronicle) Fourteen years later he opened a cricket ground of his own, in America.

The last line of the table matters most for this thesis. The man who built a digital kingdom in 2011 is now putting money into AI content. The question of who owns a face in the AI era is no longer abstract for him. Interpretation

A man who has invested in a company that can swap a face in a video is about to release a film in a year when fans are already making him with AI, without asking. That is where Part Five begins. Interpretation
