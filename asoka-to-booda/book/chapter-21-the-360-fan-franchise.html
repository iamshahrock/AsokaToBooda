Chapter 21

The 360 fan franchise

542 words · about 2 min read

Ra.One and Chennai Express were early versions of one model: a star's world run across five disciplines at once, Storytelling, Technology, Entertainment, Arts and Merchandising. The author calls it S.T.E.A.M. Eyewitness account; the author's framework A film uses one of them for a release. A franchise runs all five, all year, with the fans inside it. Interpretation

Read the career through those five disciplines and each one has a record. Interpretation

  • Storytelling. Eighty film and television titles from 1988 to 2018, the archive this campaign's poster studio uses, then three films in 2023, each sold with its own story, from the NRI lover of 1995 to the migrant of Dunki. Fact; Chapters 4 to 14
  • Technology. SRKWorld and Aryan Infomatics in 2000; Red Chillies VFX in 2006; Twitter in January 2010; Google+ in 2011; a Twitter-driven train and a missed-call line in 2013; a stake in an AI content company in 2026. Fact and Eyewitness account; Chapters 6 to 10 and 15
  • Entertainment. Temptations tours from 2004, Kaun Banega Crorepati in 2007, the Knight Riders from 2008, the SLAM! tour in 2014, and a cricket ground in Los Angeles in 2026. Fact; Chapters 7, 11 and 15
  • Arts. The illustrated G.One, its style guide and comic, and Shah Rock Jaan, the character created with the author in 2000. Eyewitness account; Chapters 6 and 9
  • Merchandising. The Hyundai Santro in 1998, the Reebok range for My Name Is Khan in 2010, and the Ra.One licensing programme in 2011. Fact; Chapters 5, 8 and 9

What no campaign ever did was run all five at once, all year, with the fans inside it. Interpretation

Agar Main King Hota, the campaign this thesis belongs to, is a working test of that model for KING. Fans make posters (arts), play story games (entertainment), earn STARKS on a public Billboard (technology), write the myth (storytelling), and animate the lairs (all of it). Fact: the platform at agarmainkinghota.com

As of October 2026 the platform runs eight sections. Fact: agarmainkinghota.com, checked live on 12 Oct 2026

  • Make Your Poster: fans pick one of his 80 film posters, cast themselves and up to two friends into its characters, and receive a new AI-painted poster in which no star's face appears.
  • King's Lairs: a story game set in six invented lairs, with Lairs Cinema, where fans animate the published set designs with their own AI accounts and their own faces.
  • Chess81: an original 9-by-9 chess variant built for the campaign.
  • Billboard: the public table of STARKS, the fans' points.
  • KING Buzz: the daily intelligence dashboard, measured against the locked 4 October 2026 baseline.
  • Asoka to Boo-Da: the author's essay, now Part Three of this book.
  • DOOM to KING: the friendly Christmas fixture of Chapter 17.
  • Inquilaab: the #FreeMyFace movement of Chapter 18.

Every page carries a Fan Wall where fans comment in up to 555 characters. Fact Each part is a working test of one of the six lessons. Whether the model works at scale is a question for the box office and the fans after 24 December 2026, and this book will report it when the numbers exist. Interpretation
