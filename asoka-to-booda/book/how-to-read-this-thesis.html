The book

How to read this thesis

129 words · about 1 min read

Three voices run through every chapter, and they are never mixed.

  • Fact: from the public record, with its source.
  • Eyewitness account: what the author saw from inside the campaigns and the years around them. The author, Shailja Gupta, co-founded SRKWorld with Shah Rukh Khan in 2000 and led the digital strategy for Ra.One and Chennai Express.
  • Interpretation: the author's reading of the evidence, marked as such.

A fourth kind of evidence appears where it applies: Archive, photographs and documents from the author's own collection, reproduced in the chapter with their dates.

A claim marked [Unverified] happened, but its exact figure, date or source is still being confirmed. It stays on the page so the reader sees what is known and what is still being checked.
