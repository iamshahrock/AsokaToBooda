KING: the hype before a frame

1,148 words · about 5 min read

Every road in this book leads to one film. Interpretation

How KING became his. It did not begin as his film. It was first planned with Sujoy Ghosh directing, Suhana Khan in the lead and her father in a cameo. Over time it grew into a Shah Rukh Khan film, and Siddharth Anand, the director of Pathaan, took over. (Fact. The Week, 18 Jan 2026) A film made to launch the daughter became the father's return. Interpretation

2 November 2025: sixty, and the title. On his sixtieth birthday the title was revealed in a one-minute video: silver hair, a deep voice, a prisoner fighting his way out of a maximum-security jail. His line was "Darr nahi, dehshat hoon": I am not fear, I am terror. (Fact. The Hollywood Reporter India, 2 Nov 2025) Thirty-two years after Darr made a stalker a star (Chapter 5), the man who played him named the next film by stepping past it. Interpretation The cast named that day ran from Deepika Padukone and Suhana Khan to Abhishek Bachchan, Anil Kapoor, Jackie Shroff, Arshad Warsi, Jaideep Ahlawat and Abhay Varma. Fact. The Hollywood Reporter India

In January 2026 trade reports put the release at Christmas, with no Hindi film set against it. Fact. The Week, 18 Jan 2026 The date was then fixed for 24 December 2026, announced with a teaser on Pathaan's third anniversary (Chapter 15). Fact. The Hollywood Reporter India

KING opens on 24 December 2026, directed by Siddharth Anand, the director of Pathaan. It brings back Deepika Padukone, casts Abhishek Bachchan as the antagonist, and gives his daughter Suhana Khan her big-screen debut. (Fact. The Hollywood Reporter India)
