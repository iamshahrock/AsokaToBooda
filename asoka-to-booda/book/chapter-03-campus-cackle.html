From 1985 to 1988 he studied economics at Hansraj College, but spent most of his time at the Theatre Action Group, learning to act from the director Barry John. He began a master's in mass communication at Jamia Millia Islamia, and left it to act. Fact

The son of a man who believed in non-violent resistance. Remember that when you reach 4 October 2021. Interpretation

The rehearsal room. The Theatre Action Group was founded in Delhi in 1973 by Barry John, a British director who had arrived in India in 1968 and stayed. (Fact. Outlook, 6 February 2022) John has staged more than 100 productions in Hindi and English, and the actors who trained with him in the TAG years include Raghuvir Yadav and Manoj Bajpayee as well as Shah Rukh Khan. (Fact. Outlook) [Unverified: the TAG plays he acted in, with years and roles, from Barry John or the group's programmes.]

What theatre gave him. Delhi theatre in the 1980s was poor, serious and collective: no stars, no close-ups, and a live audience that could walk out. Interpretation The Bombay film industry of the time trained its heroes in the mirror and the gym; he arrived trained in the rehearsal room. Interpretation Critics later compared his television work to Dilip Kumar's. (Fact. Wikipedia)

The exam he passed and the degree he did not finish. After graduating he cleared the entrance examination for the Indian Institutes of Technology, because his mother insisted. (Fact. Wikipedia) [Unverified: the year and the exam's name.] He joined the master's programme at Jamia Millia Islamia's Mass Communication Research Centre, and could not sit the final-year examination because his attendance was short. (Fact. The Tribune, 22 February 2019) The film director Kabir Khan was his junior there and studied from his notes. (Fact. Wikipedia) He is also recorded as having attended the National School of Drama. (Fact. Wikipedia) [Unverified: in what capacity; he is not listed as an NSD graduate.]

In 2018 Jamia asked the government's education ministry for permission to give its former student an honorary doctorate. The ministry declined, reportedly because he already held one from the Maulana Azad National Urdu University (2016). (Fact. The Tribune, 22 February 2019)

Mass communication. The subject he left unfinished became the work of his life. Interpretation Twelve years later he built the first personal website of an Indian film star (Chapter 6); in 2011 he ran the most connected digital campaign Indian cinema had seen (Chapter 9).
