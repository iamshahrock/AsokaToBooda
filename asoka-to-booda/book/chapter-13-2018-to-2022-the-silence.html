Chapter 13

2018 to 2022: the silence

985 words · about 4 min read

Four years without a lead release. Almost no interviews. A star who had built an empire on talking let the silence talk for him.

2019: the year without a film. He worked, but off screen. Red Chillies produced Bard of Blood for Netflix, and in October 2019 he sat with David Letterman for Netflix's My Next Guest Needs No Introduction. (Fact. American Bazaar, 10 Oct 2019) On his 54th birthday, 2 November 2019, the crowd at his gate waited for an announcement that did not come. He told them: "The day it will be announced will be our birthday." (Fact. India TV) The only stage he kept was the balcony. Interpretation

But silence is not absence. The fan clubs kept the birthday at the gate, trended the old films and built their own campaigns. The brand was held by the people who loved it. Interpretation

Nor was the man idle. When the pandemic reached Mumbai in April 2020, he and Gauri Khan offered their four-storey personal office to the city corporation as a quarantine facility for women, children and the elderly. His Meer Foundation committed daily food for more than 5,500 families, and his companies gave to the PM-CARES Fund and the Maharashtra relief fund. (Fact. The Tribune, 4 Apr 2020) He did it without a campaign. (Interpretation)

Red Chillies kept producing for streaming, including the Netflix horror series Betaal. (Fact. India.com) The studio, the team and the foundation kept working. Only the star stayed off screen. Interpretation

What the company made while the star was away. Between Zero and Pathaan, Red Chillies released Badla (2019, ₹138.49 crore worldwide), Bard of Blood (Netflix, 27 September 2019), Kaamyaab (2020), Betaal and Class of '83 (Netflix, 2020), Bob Biswas (ZEE5, 2021), and Love Hostel (ZEE5) and Darlings (Netflix) in 2022. (Fact. Room · ArclightZ, from Wikipedia: Red Chillies Entertainment) Eight titles in four years, none of them starring him. Fact, count The silence was the actor's, not the company's. Interpretation

The cricket kept winning. In the Caribbean, the Trinbago Knight Riders, which his Knight Riders Group bought in 2015, won the 2020 Caribbean Premier League without losing a match: twelve played, twelve won. (Fact. Wikipedia: Trinbago Knight Riders) It was the club's fourth title, after 2015, 2017 and 2018; a fifth followed in 2025. Fact. as above In the year of the pandemic and no film, a team he owned had a perfect season. Interpretation

On 2 October 2021, Gandhi Jayanti, a raid on a cruise ship bound for Goa ended with Shah Rukh Khan's son taken into custody. He was arrested the next day. On 4 October he was produced before a magistrate and remanded. (Fact. Free Press Journal timeline)

That day, 4 October, there was not a single fan outside Mannat standing in silence to speak for him. Eyewitness account

Not one, at the gate of the most celebrated house in India. Every year on 2 November, crowds fill that same road for one wave from the balcony. That day the road was quiet.

The young man spent about four weeks in custody. He was granted bail on 28 October and came home on 30 October. On 27 May 2022, the investigating agency's chargesheet did not name him, citing a lack of evidence. Fact. Same source.

In order: bail was refused by a magistrate's court on 8 October and by the Special NDPS Court on 20 October. The Bombay High Court granted it on 28 October, and he came home on 30 October. Fact. Free Press Journal timeline; the author's essay, Act III Through all of it, the most quoted man in India said almost nothing in public. Interpretation What does it say about due process when a young man spends four weeks in custody in a case that later finds no evidence against him? Interpretation; the author's question, Act III

What came after matters as much as what happened. On 27 May 2022 the Narcotics Control Bureau filed its complaint against 14 people. It did not file against six others, his son among them, "due to lack of sufficient evidence", after a Special Investigation Team review. (Fact. The News Minute, 27 May 2022)

2022: the line held by others. Through the four years, the fan clubs did not wait for permission. They kept the birthday at the gate of his home in Mumbai, they trended his old films, they built their own campaigns. The brand was being held by the people who loved it. Interpretation; the author's essay, Act IV When he finally returned to the screen in 2022, it was in small parts in other people's films, a guest in Rocketry and Brahmastra, before his own film was ready. (Fact. Gulf News) Was the silence a wound, or a weapon? What did he see from the balcony, every birthday, that the industry did not? The author's questions, Act IV

A year later, after his return, the story turned. On 12 May 2023 the Central Bureau of Investigation registered a case against Sameer Wankhede, who had headed the bureau's Mumbai office during the arrest, and four others. It alleged that they sought a ₹25 crore bribe for not framing Aryan Khan, and searched 29 locations. (Fact. The Week, 12 May 2023) These are allegations, not findings. Wankhede responded: "I am getting rewarded for being a patriot", and said the assets found were acquired before he joined the service. (Fact. The Week, 14 May 2023)

Through all of it, from the arrest to the bribery case, Shah Rukh Khan made no public statement on the case. [Unverified: to confirm against the record]

What does a father do with that kind of humiliation? And what does a showman do when he decides not to perform?

He waits. And when he comes back, he does not ask to be seen.
