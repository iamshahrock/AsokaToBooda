Chapter 12

Zero: the pause before the pivot

1,212 words · about 5 min read

Five years after the train, he made the most ambitious film of the run, and the story around it was the most inventive since 2013. Interpretation

A title on New Year's Day. The title was revealed on 1 January 2018 in a teaser set to a Mohammed Rafi song; it passed three million YouTube views in under 24 hours. An Eid teaser on 13 June 2018 paired him with Salman Khan. The trailer, launched in November, reached 100 million YouTube views faster than any Indian film before it. (Fact. Wikipedia: Zero) Directed by Aanand L. Rai and produced by Gauri Khan with Rai's Colour Yellow, the film cast him as a man about three feet tall, made possible by Red Chillies VFX, which won the Filmfare Award for Best Special Effects for it. Fact. Wikipedia: Zero The effects house founded in 2006 (Chapter 7) had made its most difficult hero yet. Interpretation

Zero, in December 2018, was the most ambitious film of the run: a star playing Bauua Singh, a short man from Meerut with an outsized ego, in a story that went from small-town romance to space. The campaign gave Bauua his own digital life, with a WhatsApp sticker pack, a Snapchat story, an Amazon Alexa skill billed as a first for a Bollywood film, a Twitter handle written by the film's writer, and Bauua Ki Toli, a points game whose top fans met the cast. (Fact. exchange4media, 19 December 2018)

Six years in the making. Aanand L. Rai conceived the film in 2012 and began pre-production in 2016. It went through several working titles, among them Katrina Meri Jaan, before Zero was announced in January 2018. (Fact. Wikipedia: Zero) Shooting began in Mumbai in May 2017 and ended in Orlando in June 2018: about 200 shooting days against a plan of 140. (Fact. Wikipedia)

How to shrink a star. To make him about three feet tall, the film used forced perspective, pits in the floor that were removed in post-production, and ramps in place of stairs, and its makers cited the methods of The Lord of the Rings. (Fact. Wikipedia) Room · ArclightZ records the result: Red Chillies VFX's Filmfare award. Fact No Indian star of his rank had made himself physically small for a whole film. Interpretation [Unverified: a survey of Indian leading men in comparable roles would confirm or correct this.]

The cast. Anushka Sharma played Aafia, a space scientist with cerebral palsy; Katrina Kaif played Babita, a film star with a drinking problem; Abhay Deol and R. Madhavan had supporting roles. (Fact. Wikipedia) The cameos amounted to a roll call of his career: Salman Khan, Kajol, Rani Mukerji, Juhi Chawla, Karisma Kapoor, Deepika Padukone, Alia Bhatt and Jaya Bachchan. Sridevi, who had died in February 2018, appeared posthumously. (Fact. Wikipedia; Sridevi's death is public record) A film about a small man surrounded by stars was itself surrounded by them. Interpretation

The music. Ajay–Atul composed the songs, with lyrics by Irshad Kamil and Kumaar; the singles included "Mere Naam Tu", "Issaqbaazi" and "Husn Parcham", with "Heer Badnaam" by Tanishk Bagchi added the day before release. (Fact. Wikipedia)

The opening. It opened on about 4,380 screens in India and 1,585 abroad, 5,965 in all, the third-widest Hindi release of 2018 after Thugs of Hindostan and Race 3. (Fact. Taran Adarsh, via Wikipedia) Day one in India took ₹18.78 crore; the three-day weekend about ₹53 crore. (Fact. Box Office India, via Wikipedia)

The reviews. Mixed to negative. Rotten Tomatoes recorded 31% positive from 16 reviews. Critics praised the effects and Katrina Kaif, and faulted the plot and the second half. Baradwaj Rangan and Raja Sen gave it 3.5 out of 5; Saibal Chatterjee of NDTV gave it 2 and called it a mess; Anna MM Vetticad of Firstpost gave it 1.5. (Fact. Wikipedia) At the 64th Filmfare Awards it had seven nominations, including Best Actor, and won one, for special effects. (Fact. Wikipedia)

It opened on 21 December 2018. Its budget was given as ₹200 crore after post-production, against earlier reports of ₹150 crore. Its worldwide gross is given as ₹178 crore by one tracker and about ₹191 crore by another, with about ₹87 crore in India and US$14 million abroad. Either way it was one of the biggest failures of his career. Fact. Filmibeat, 2023; Wikipedia: Zero, citing Box Office India; figures differ by source

Why it failed: what the record supports. The record shows a long, expensive shoot; reviews that liked the effects and disliked the story; and an opening weekend of about ₹53 crore in India for a ₹200 crore film. Fact. as above It does not show one single cause. The common explanations, that audiences did not want to see the star diminished, or that the space-scientist second half lost the small-town audience the first half had won, are opinions. Interpretation [Unverified: audience research or exit polling from the distributors.]

What it cost, and what it bought. It cost the most visible years of his career. It also bought him the one thing a star at the top rarely gets: a reason to stop. Interpretation In 2019 he said in Beijing that he might have made the wrong film; four years later he came back with three films in a year (Chapter 14). Fact, as above; Interpretation Room · Advantage shows the same curve in the market: his brand valuation fell to 10th in 2022 and rose to 1st by 2025. Fact. Room · Advantage

China. Zero closed the Beijing International Film Festival in April 2019 and was released in China in March 2019. (Fact. Wikipedia) [Unverified: the Chinese release date, which the source places before the festival, and its Chinese gross, from Maoyan or another Chinese ticketing source.]

Four months later, in Beijing, where Zero closed the 2019 Beijing International Film Festival, he told CGTN: "Maybe I made the wrong film." He had not decided what to do next, he said, and "if it doesn't excite you in the morning, you should not wake up". (Fact. Gulf News / IANS, 19 Apr 2019) Then he did something no one expected: he stopped. No new lead role in a released film for more than four years. Fact; the next release was Pathaan, January 2023

In the language of this thesis, Zero is the 0 in Zero to One: the lowest point, and the start of the climb. Interpretation
