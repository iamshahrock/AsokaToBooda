What the list shows. Until 2017 he spoke everywhere: magazines, chat shows, universities. From 2018, after Zero, he gave almost no film interviews; when he came back in 2023, he answered fans directly on social media and let the films speak. Interpretation; see Chapters 13 and 14 The stages also changed. In the 1990s it was Indian magazines; after 2012 it was Yale, TED, Davos, Netflix and Locarno. He went from the most quoted star in India to an Indian voice in global forums. Interpretation

Tall Tales. The 2000 website's name for this room promised exaggeration, and he has always played with it: the jokes about his looks, his money and his own legend are part of how he talks. Interpretation This book quotes him only where a source records the words. Method note
