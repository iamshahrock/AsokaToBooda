The Wall II · My Name Is Khan, 2010: the star becomes the platform

1,262 words · about 5 min read

January 2010: the line reopens. Ten years after SRKWorld, he opened a direct line to his audience again, and this time he did not have to build it. He joined Twitter as @iamsrk at the start of January 2010; his first tweet, on 2 or 3 January by different accounts, began: "Being extremely shy I never thought I would be here." Karan Johar had talked him into it. (Fact. IBTimes; Siasat) Five years later the account had 10.7 million followers. Fact. IBTimes The shy man who had built a website in 2000 now had a microphone in every fan's pocket, and he used it himself. Interpretation

The first film he sold through it was the most personal of his career. My Name Is Khan, directed by Karan Johar, was the story of Rizwan Khan, a Muslim man with Asperger's syndrome who crosses post-9/11 America to tell its president one sentence: "My name is Khan, and I am not a terrorist." It was the kind of film that could have stayed a festival title. Its campaign made it an international release. Interpretation

The deal. On 7 August 2009 he signed a worldwide distribution deal with Fox Star Studios, reported at ₹100 crore; unconfirmed estimates ran from ₹81 crore to ₹100 crore, and Fox did not disclose the figure. Fox Star distributed in India, Fox Searchlight in the United States and 20th Century Fox elsewhere; Abu Dhabi's Image Nation joined in February 2010. (Fact. Wikipedia: My Name Is Khan; Filmibeat, 2 Sept 2009) A Hollywood studio was buying a Hindi film for the world. Interpretation

The launch, on every front at once. The first look was unveiled in Mumbai and broadcast across 70 countries on the Star TV network. The trailer debuted with Avatar, then became the first in India to run as a roadblock: three minutes on all the leading channels at the same moment. Fact. Wikipedia: My Name Is Khan; this confirms what the earlier draft marked unverified On 1 February 2010 he and Kajol became the first Indian film stars to ring the NASDAQ opening bell in New York. They were there to launch a film, not to list a company. (Fact. Deccan Herald; Wikipedia) The image said what the campaign was built to say: this film was a global event, and its star was a global property. Interpretation

Brands carried the story into shops. Reebok launched an MNIK range on 10 February 2010, led by the "Winning Stride" shoe his character wears in the film, priced from ₹999 to ₹5,999. (Fact. FashionUnited) Reebok and Samsonite made co-branded commercials, beside tie-ups with Century Ply, DishTV, Bharti Airtel, MakeMyTrip and Kyazoonga. (Fact. exchange4media, 3 Feb 2010) The DishTV tie-up let fans win a meeting with the star at Mannat. Fact. exchange4media In London he sat on Friday Night with Jonathan Ross. Fact. Wikipedia

On 10 February the film premiered in the UAE. Fact. Wikipedia In Berlin it opened out of competition at the 60th International Film Festival; screening tickets auctioned on eBay at £1,000 each sold out in five seconds. (Fact. Wikipedia; Filmibeat, 2 Feb 2010)

The year a city stood behind him. At home, Shiv Sena called for a boycott until he apologised for saying Pakistani cricketers should have been picked for the IPL. Police in Mumbai arrested 1,023 Shiv Sena activists in 24 hours and guarded 63 theatres; the state deployed five battalions of its reserve police. (Fact. Governance Now, 10 Feb 2010; Wikipedia) He did not apologise. On 12 February the city answered: the first multiplex shows sold out within hours, and the film opened to full houses across India. (Fact. Al Jazeera, 12 Feb 2010; Wikipedia)

What it earned. The film opened to about ₹29.5 crore net in India over its first weekend, then a February record. Abroad it broke records: the biggest overseas opening for an Indian film, the first to gross more than ₹100 crore overseas, about US$23.5 million in all, the highest-grossing Bollywood film in the UK and the Middle East, and about £2.6 million in Britain alone, the year's highest-grossing foreign-language film there. In the United States it opened in 120 theatres, a record for an Indian film, and took US$1.99 million in its first weekend. Its final worldwide gross was about ₹223 crore. Fact. Wikipedia: My Name Is Khan; budget estimates differ, ₹55 crore by Mint and ₹85 crore in other listings It won three Filmfare Awards, including his eighth for Best Actor. Fact. Newark, 14 August 2009. A week after the Fox deal, he flew into Newark airport, New Jersey, to promote the film. Immigration officers took him aside and questioned him for over an hour; he said it was at least two. He was released after officials of the Indian consulate intervened, and a US Customs and Border Protection spokesperson called the questioning routine. (Fact. Wikipedia: My Name Is Khan, citing the BBC) Six months before the release of a film whose hero is stopped and questioned because of his name, its star was stopped and questioned at an American airport. Interpretation The incident gave the campaign a real-world story that no marketing budget could have bought; this book records it as an event, not as a plan. Interpretation

Playing Rizwan. Karan Johar and the writer Shibani Bathija researched Asperger's syndrome for about two years and met couples who had written about it. (Fact. Wikipedia) He read The Curious Incident of the Dog in the Night-Time and spent ten days in a bathroom practising a fixed, expressionless gaze. (Fact. Wikipedia) Critics divided on the result: Taran Adarsh called it his career-best performance, The Hindu praised how unlaboured it looked, and NPR's Mark Jenkins found the portrayal of the condition dubious. (Fact. Wikipedia)

The verdicts. The New York Times' Rachel Saltz wrote that it taught lessons about Islam and tolerance while drawing tears; The Hollywood Reporter's Kirk Honeycutt said it examined anti-Muslim hysteria in America compellingly; Variety's Jay Weissberg called it overstuffed but enjoyable. (Fact. Wikipedia) Rotten Tomatoes recorded 86% positive from 29 reviews; Metacritic 50 out of 100 from seven. (Fact. Wikipedia) At the 56th Filmfare Awards, on 29 January 2011, it won three: Best Actor for him, Best Actress for Kajol and Best Director for Karan Johar. (Fact. Wikipedia: 56th Filmfare Awards) Best Film went to Dabangg. Fact. as above The Western reviews matter for this book: the leading American papers reviewed one of his films as a statement about America itself. Interpretation

The diaspora that DDLJ had discovered in 1995 had become, fifteen years later, the market that decided whether a Hindi film was a global hit. Interpretation

In 2010 a city queued around the block to stand behind him. Eleven years later, at his own gate, by the author's own account, nobody came. That contrast is the question at the heart of this thesis. Interpretation

What changed in 2010. Until then, the star was the face of a campaign. With My Name Is Khan he became its platform, an actor, cultural ambassador, commercial partner and media property at the same time. A film has a release date. A platform keeps producing experiences, products and stories long after the opening weekend. Interpretation

That difference became the engine of the next three years. The next film would not use a platform. It would try to build one.
