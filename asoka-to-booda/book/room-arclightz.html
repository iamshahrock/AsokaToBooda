A room for the producer. Asoka (2001) was credited to Dreamz Unlimited and Archlightz Films, with him and Juhi Chawla as producers. (Fact. Wikipedia: Asoka) From Dreamz Unlimited in 1999 to Red Chillies, its VFX house and its streaming series, the producer's record runs beside the actor's. Interpretation

Three companies. Dreamz Unlimited was founded in 1999 by Shah Rukh Khan, Juhi Chawla and the director Aziz Mirza, and co-produced three films between 2000 and 2003. (Fact. Wikipedia: Shah Rukh Khan) Red Chillies Entertainment was founded in 2002 by Shah Rukh Khan and Gauri Khan as its successor. (Fact. Wikipedia: Red Chillies Entertainment) Asoka also carried a third banner, Arclightz & Films; the website's producer page of 2000 was called Arc Lights. (Fact. Wikipedia: Red Chillies Entertainment; Archive: the author's collection) Gauri Khan is co-chairperson of Red Chillies and the producer of record on nearly all its films. (Fact. Wikipedia: Gauri Khan)

How to read the tables. The budget and gross figures are those published on each film's Wikipedia page as of October 2026, in Indian rupees (1 crore = 10 million). Where the page gives two figures, both are shown. A gross is ticket sales, not the producer's profit. N/A means no figure was published on the page. Fact; method note

Red Chillies VFX. Founded in 2006 and led by Haresh Hingorani and Keitan Yadav, with more than 130 staff, the largest of the group's companies. Its work includes Chak De! India, Krrish 3 and Don as well as Red Chillies' own films. (Fact. Wikipedia: Red Chillies Entertainment) Ra.One won the National Film Award and the Filmfare Award for special effects, and Zero and Jawan won Filmfare's visual-effects award. (Fact. The cricket. In 2008 he, Juhi Chawla and Jay Mehta bought the Kolkata franchise of the Indian Premier League for US$75.09 million and named it Kolkata Knight Riders. (Fact. Wikipedia: Shah Rukh Khan) Red Chillies holds 55% of the owning Knight Riders Group, with the Mehta Group. (Fact. Wikipedia: Red Chillies Entertainment) The team won the IPL in 2012, 2014 and 2024; the group also owns the Trinbago Knight Riders in the Caribbean Premier League. (Fact. Wikipedia: Shah Rukh Khan)

In 2015 the Knight Riders Group bought the majority of the Caribbean Premier League franchise in Trinidad and Tobago, renamed it the Trinbago Knight Riders in 2016 and handed its operations to Red Chillies. It has won five titles, the most in the league's history: 2015, 2017, 2018, 2020 (unbeaten, 12 wins from 12) and 2025. (Fact. Wikipedia: Trinbago Knight Riders) The Knight Riders Cricket Ground in Los Angeles followed in 2026 (Chapter 15). [Unverified: the dates the group entered Major League Cricket in the US and the ILT20 in the UAE, from the leagues' records.] With the IPL titles of 2012, 2014 and 2024, his teams have won eight league titles, in India and the Caribbean. Fact, count; Interpretation

The money. Red Chillies does not publish annual accounts to the public in a form this book has found. A June 2024 Times Now report was headlined "Red Chillies Declares Rs 85 Crore Profit For FY23". (Fact. cited on Wikipedia: Red Chillies Entertainment) [Unverified: revenue and profit by year, from the company's filings with the Registrar of Companies.]

What the record shows. The producer lost money first. Three Dreamz films grossed about ₹97 crore between them, and the company closed. Fact, from the table; Interpretation Twenty years later, a single Red Chillies film, Jawan, grossed more than ₹1,148 crore. Fact, from the table In between, the company built what the actor could not buy: its own effects house, a cricket team, and a streaming slate made for Netflix. Interpretation The actor's career has falls (Chapters 6 and 12); the producer's record shows a business that kept building through both. Interpretation
