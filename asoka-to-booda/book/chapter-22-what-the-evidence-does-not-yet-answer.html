Chapter 22

What the evidence does not yet answer

234 words · about 1 min read

The research agenda this thesis leaves open:

  • What exactly happened outside Mannat on 4 October 2021, hour by hour? Photographs, news footage and police records would settle it.
  • Did the events of 2021 change how he approaches public appearances, marketing and business? Only he can answer that.
  • What did Ra.One's merchandise and licensing programme actually sell? The FICCI-KPMG 2012 report, read in full, gives no count; the licensees' records and the author's own files hold the numbers.
  • How many fan-made AI works about KING exist across all platforms, and how many views do they hold? The daily log is a floor; a full census needs platform data.
  • Who owns a star's face in the AI era, and who should be paid when it is used? The courts have not answered. The fans have started to.

And four questions only the next months can answer:

  • How does KING open on 24 December 2026, and how does it play beside Avengers: Doomsday?
  • Will the star speak before release, or let the silence sell a fourth film?
  • Will any studio, his own included, license a star's face to fans on fair terms, as the Inquilaab demands ask?
  • What will the investigating agencies and courts finally record in the cases that followed October 2021?

This book will add the answers as they arrive, labelled Fact, and leave the rest open. Interpretation
