Chapter 22

What the evidence does not yet answer

706 words · about 3 min read

The research agenda this thesis leaves open:

  • What exactly happened outside Mannat on 4 October 2021, hour by hour? Photographs, news footage and police records would settle it.
  • Did the events of 2021 change how he approaches public appearances, marketing and business? Only he can answer that.
  • What did Ra.One's merchandise and licensing programme actually sell? The FICCI-KPMG 2012 report, read in full, gives no count; the licensees' records and the author's own files hold the numbers.
  • How many fan-made AI works about KING exist across all platforms, and how many views do they hold? The daily log is a floor; a full census needs platform data.
  • Who owns a star's face in the AI era, and who should be paid when it is used? The courts have not answered. The fans have started to.

And four questions only the next months can answer:

  • How does KING open on 24 December 2026, and how does it play beside Avengers: Doomsday?
  • Will the star speak before release, or let the silence sell a fourth film?
  • Will any studio, his own included, license a star's face to fans on fair terms, as the Inquilaab demands ask?
  • What will the investigating agencies and courts finally record in the cases that followed October 2021?

Where each question stands, October 2026. For every open question, here is what the record holds today and what would close it. Method note

4 October 2021 at Mannat. The record today is one eyewitness account, the author's, that the gate stood almost empty on the day his son was remanded (Chapter 13). Later reports describe fans gathering on other days. Eyewitness account; Fact, as cited in Chapter 13 What would close it: dated photographs or news footage of the gate on that day, and police deployment records for Bandstand. Until then the book keeps the account, labelled. Method note

What the events of 2021 changed. The pattern is on the record: no lead release between December 2018 and January 2023, and almost no interviews around Pathaan, Jawan and Dunki (Chapters 13 and 14). Fact Whether the silence was a choice, a strategy or a wound, only he can say. This book will not guess. Method note

Ra.One's licensing numbers. The record today: an online store, a style guide, a graphic novel and a game, reported by FICCI-KPMG in 2012; the figure of 64 merchandise items remains unconfirmed (Chapter 9 and Appendix D). Fact; Unverified, as listed What would close it: the licensees' sales reports and the campaign's own licensing files.

The census of fan-made AI work. The campaign's daily scan had found and linked 40 fan-made AI works about KING by 18 October 2026; the 21 that show public view counts had 2,883,120 views between them. Fact: the campaign's log, data/ai-creator-log.json, 18 Oct 2026 This is a floor: the scan reads public posts on four platforms and cannot see private shares or platforms it does not search. A full census needs the platforms' own data. Method note

Who owns a star's face. The record today is a run of court orders protecting named actors one by one, from Amitabh Bachchan in November 2022 to the orders of February 2026, and amended IT Rules in force from 20 February 2026 that require AI content to be labelled and taken down within three hours on notice (Chapter 18). Fact, as cited in Chapter 18 This book found no public record of Shah Rukh Khan seeking such an order. [Unverified: as of October 2026; Appendix D] This book found no Indian film star with a licensed AI-likeness programme open to fans (Chapter 20). Interpretation, from a search result

Does the fan franchise work at scale? The platform's first logged week, 11 to 18 October 2026, counted 54 unique visitors, 153 page views, 17 posters and 2,121 STARKS (Chapter 21). Fact: the platform's database The question is what those numbers become between his birthday on 2 November and the release on 24 December. Method note

This book will add the answers as they arrive, labelled Fact, and leave the rest open. Interpretation
