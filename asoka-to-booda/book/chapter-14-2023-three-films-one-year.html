Chapter 14

2023: three films, one year

1,124 words · about 5 min read

He waited four years. When he came back, he did not ask to be seen. Interpretation

The comeback began on his birthday. On 2 November 2022 the Pathaan teaser arrived (Sacnilk), and the campaign that followed broke every rule of the playbook he had written: almost no interviews, no media tour, no explaining. The film, the music, the fans and one man on a balcony.

December 2022: one more boycott. The first song, "Besharam Rang", drew protests over a saffron-coloured bikini; a Madhya Pradesh minister called it objectionable, effigies were burned, and on 4 January 2023 Bajrang Dal members tore down posters at an Ahmedabad multiplex. The censor board asked for changes, but the scene stayed, and Gujarat's home minister promised cinemas police protection. (Fact. Wikipedia: Pathaan) It was the third boycott of the book, after My Name Is Khan in 2010 and Dilwale in 2015. This time he did not answer at all. Interpretation

The trade noticed. On 17 January 2023, eight days before release, the analyst Taran Adarsh pointed out that the film had given "NO interviews to media persons during pre-release promotions", relying on songs and trailers instead. (Fact. Social Samosa, Jan 2023)

But the star did talk, only not to the press. From the day he announced the film, 2 March 2022, he ran #AskSRK: short question-and-answer sessions of about 15 minutes on his own Twitter account, where more than 43 million followers could ask him anything. (Fact. The Quint) Fans filled the gap the press left: blood donation camps, life-size cutouts, house-full first shows. On 25 January 2023 Pathaan crossed ₹100 crore worldwide on its first day. Fact. Social Samosa

25 January 2023: Pathaan. The trailer, released on 10 January, was projected on the Burj Khalifa on 14 January. Fact. Wikipedia: Pathaan The film, directed by Siddharth Anand for Yash Raj Films on a budget of about ₹250 crore, opened on 25 January on about 8,000 screens, 5,500 of them in India. It took ₹106 crore worldwide on its first day, the first Hindi film to cross ₹100 crore in a day, and became the first Hindi film to earn ₹500 crore net in India. Fact. Wikipedia: Pathaan, citing Bollywood Hungama Its final worldwide gross is given as ₹1,050 crore by Bollywood Hungama and ₹1,055 crore by The Sentinel. Fact; figures differ by source

This was not silence. It was the founder returning as the medium, in a new form: no intermediaries, no media tour, only the star and his fans, face to face, at scale. It was the Chennai Express lesson, rewritten for 2023. Interpretation

What Pathaan broke. It opened on about 8,000 screens worldwide, 5,500 of them in India, and took ₹106 crore worldwide on its first day: ₹70 crore in India and ₹36.69 crore abroad. It was the first Hindi film to gross ₹100 crore worldwide in a day. (Fact. Wikipedia: Pathaan) It went on to become the fastest Hindi film to ₹200, ₹300 and ₹400 crore in India; the first original Hindi film to net more than ₹400 crore at home, then the first to net ₹500 crore; and, on its 37th day, the highest-grossing Hindi film in India, passing Baahubali 2. (Fact. Wikipedia) It was a Yash Raj Films production, directed by Siddharth Anand, on a reported budget of ₹250 crore, and grossed ₹1,050.30 crore worldwide. (Fact. Wikipedia; the page also gives ₹1,050.50 crore) Every one of those records was set by a man who had given no interview about the film. Interpretation; see Room · Tall Tales

7 September 2023: Jawan. The second film of the year was his own. Jawan, directed by Atlee in his Hindi debut and produced by Gauri Khan for Red Chillies on a budget of about ₹300 crore, opened on 7 September 2023 with ₹75.4 crore net in India, a record for a Hindi film. Its pre-release event was held in Chennai on 30 August, at a college, not a press hall. (Fact. Wikipedia: Jawan) It grossed ₹1,138 crore worldwide by its sixth week by one count and more than ₹1,148 crore by November by another, the highest-grossing Indian film of 2023 and the highest-grossing Hindi film in India. (Fact. Madhyamam; Wikipedia: Jawan, citing Bollywood Hungama) Two years later, on 1 August 2025, it won him his first National Film Award for Best Actor. Fact. Wikipedia: Jawan

21 December 2023: Dunki. The third was Rajkumar Hirani's Dunki, a story of migrants taking the illegal "donkey route" abroad. Its first teaser, "Drop 1", came out on 2 November 2023, his 58th birthday. (Fact. Wikipedia: Dunki; The Quint, 2 Nov 2023) It grossed about ₹410 crore worldwide in 13 days, and finished at about ₹454 crore by Sacnilk's count and ₹470.6 crore by Bollywood Hungama's. Fact. Two films past ₹1,000 crore in one year, and a third past ₹400 crore. Dunki even kept the birthday ritual: its first "drop", the teaser, came out on 2 November 2023. (Fact. The Quint, 2 Nov 2023)

The man everyone had written off had not lost his audience. He had stopped asking for it, and it came anyway. Silence had become scarcity, and scarcity had become an event. Interpretation

Was this a comeback, or had he simply never left? If silence can sell a ₹1,000-crore film, what can the showman do when he speaks again? (The author's questions; the essay Asoka to Boo-Da*, Act V)*

The answer came in the next three years, not in films but in everything around them. Interpretation

2023 in one sum. Pathaan ₹1,050.30 crore, Jawan ₹1,148.32 crore and Dunki about ₹470.6 crore: about ₹2,669 crore worldwide from three films in eleven months.
