Vision & Mission

Why this site exists.

Our Vision

Agar Main King Hota — if I were the King — is the question every Shah Rukh Khan fan has asked at least once. This site is where that dream becomes something you can see, make and share: a fan-built world around KING, where Bauua's ZERO meets the King's ONE, and where more than three decades of love for Shah Rukh Khan turn into posters, games, stories and conversations made by the fans themselves. KING by FANS is not a campaign you watch; it is one you enter.

Our Mission

To give every fan, anywhere in the world, a place to take part all the way to KING's release on 24 December 2026 and beyond: make your own KING poster, play the King's Lairs and Chess81, follow the daily KING Buzz, read Asoka to Boo-Da, and earn STARKS for everything you create. And because every King was once a fan, the site answers back with Agar Main Fan Hota, the King's own Playroom. Made with love by fans, for the King.
