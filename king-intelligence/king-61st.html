The day, hour by hour

  1. 1 Nov · 14:12 ISTSRK posts the Bandra-Worli Sea Link lit with his face, "2 DAYS TO GO", "with Google Gemini". ↗ Official · Birthday eve: Mannat turns into a screen
  2. 2 Nov · 00:00 ISTNo midnight balcony. The giant screen on Mannat shows his handwritten note in neon: "Thank you all for your wishes and love. Sending mine right back to you all. Lots of love and a big hug." Mannat is under scaffolding for a rebuild and the family has been living elsewhere. Reported
  3. 2 Nov · 00:09 ISTTu Mil Jaaye goes live on YouTube. ↗ Official
  4. 2 Nov · 00:11 ISTSRK's reel: "Yeh Daur Ishq ka Naseeb Waalon ko milta hai... Jahan Barood ki Khushboo mein Gulab khilta hai... Of Love and other Battles....This December, #LoveLikeAKing" ↗ Official
  5. 2 Nov · through the dayFans gather outside Mannat; police set barricades about 500 metres away and check commuters, with security tightened after a recent shooting incident involving Tiger Shroff. Fan clubs screen Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge, donate stationery and groceries to an orphanage, run free health check-ups and hold a party in Juhu. Reported · PTI
  6. 2 Nov · through the dayFilm friends post wishes, most of them naming KING: Karan Johar, Farah Khan, Ajay Devgn, Anil Kapoor, Shilpa Shetty, Riteish and Genelia Deshmukh, Sonam Kapoor, Manish Malhotra, Atlee. Himesh Reshammiya calls the song "superb" under SRK's post. Reported
  7. 2 Nov · eveningDubai's Burj Khalifa carries his image with "WE LOVE YOU SRK", as it has on past birthdays. Reported
  8. 2 Nov · nightfallHe comes out. On the Mannat balcony, on a specially built platform, in a white T-shirt, beanie and jeans: he waves, bows, folds his hands and opens his arms as the crowd chants his name. Reported · PTI, India TV
  9. 2 Nov · late nightThe party goes on outside Mannat and he is back on the balcony: he blows kisses and strikes the signature pose. News channels post the footage through the night (India Today 22:34 IST, CNBC-TV18, zoom). Fan channels put the crowd at 100,000; no official count.
