Data discipline

Verified/public: directly supported by identifiable public sources. Tracked/internal: carried forward from the intelligence dataset already assembled in this chat. Unavailable: never invented. Posts are not people. Platform followers are not summed into unique fans. Cumulative views are not substituted for launch-window views. Where datasets are not comparable, the dashboard says so.
