↺ Every use pays a share back to the well: the star and the IP owner earn from the fans' creativity instead of fighting it.

The question Interpretation

When oil was discovered, it did not just fuel lamps. It created cars, roads, mechanics, inventors and a whole new playground. Fans are already drilling for this new oil, in the dark: unlicensed, legally grey, with nothing flowing back to the people whose faces and stories they use, and with lawsuits waiting for the companies whose tools they use.

So why are the AI generators, the billion-dollar tech companies and the IP owners not building the reserve together, officially, with the stars on board? A licensed reserve turns a grey market into a real one, and gives millions of storytellers a legal place to create.

What makes a reserve safe to open

Consent. The star decides what is in the reserve and what is not.

Revenue share. Every licensed use pays the star and the IP owner.

Labels. Official and fan-made work are clearly marked, so nobody is fooled.

Limits. No misuse: no deceptive deepfakes, no harmful or explicit use.

Credit. Fan creators are named and can earn from their work too.

A framework like this is what fans can ask for together: an AI law for fans, written with the stars rather than around them.

Data discipline

  1. 01Verified / public: directly supported by identifiable public sources.
  2. 02Tracked / internal: carried forward from the intelligence dataset already assembled; labelled as such.
  3. 03Unavailable: never invented. Shown as Not captured or N/A.
  4. 04Posts are not people. Platform followers are not summed into unique fans.
  5. 05Cumulative views are not substituted for launch-window views. Where datasets are not comparable, the dashboard says so.
  6. 0604 Oct 2026 is the immutable baseline. Daily records are appended; historical records are never overwritten.
  7. 07Registration is not attendance. Announcement is not execution.
  8. 08No invented composite scores. Longitudinal comparative evidence is not replaced by a single index.

Immutable baseline. The original 04 Oct values (699,000 #srkking posts, 31.176M sampled video attention, 12.973M sampled viral peak) are not overwritten. 05 Oct tracked movement (742,400 posts, 38.720M video attention, 16.210M peak) remains historical; 07 Oct public source snapshots are added alongside.
