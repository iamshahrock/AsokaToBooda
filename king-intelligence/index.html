Data integrity / methodology

  • Measured facts: observed follower counts, public hashtag snapshot, sampled Reel views and dated source observations.
  • Sampled observations: top discussions, surfaced lines, qualitative sentiment and visual attention concentration.
  • Analytical inference: what those signals may imply for marketing, fan participation and cultural attention.
  • Unknown / not measured: unique people, true global reach, demographic distribution, geographic percentages, population sentiment, monetary value and causal impact.
  • No invented percentages: sentiment is not assigned a fake numerical share; geography is not assigned a fake country split.
  • Reproducibility: future daily reports should preserve platform, query, timestamp, sample size and counting method.
