Audience size is not cultural imprint.A larger account can have lower interaction density.
Trailer views are not fandom.Launch velocity, repeat engagement and organised fan behaviour need separate measurement.
Fan-site followers are not unique fans.People can belong to multiple communities.
Geography is activation infrastructure.City-level organised fandom can matter more to campaign execution than aggregate reach.
