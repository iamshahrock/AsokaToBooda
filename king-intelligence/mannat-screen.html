A projector test pattern lights up SRK's home the night before he turns 61

A giant white screen now wraps the new tower that rises behind Mannat's heritage bungalow in Bandra. Fan videos shot on the night of 31 Oct show exactly what it is for: a projector calibration grid, a circle and red, green, blue and white colour bars thrown across the whole face of the building, with a projector rig and crew on the bungalow's roof. That is how a projection-mapping show is lined up before it runs.

Nobody official has said what will play on it, or when. Fan accounts say it is a KING teaser on 2 November, and the press has repeated that as a question, not a fact. The family has been living at Pali Hill while Mannat is rebuilt, so the famous balcony greeting is not expected this year.Reported

At 14:12 IST today SRK posted the other half of the birthday light show himself: the Bandra-Worli Sea Link's pylon lit with his face, "2 DAYS TO GO" and "with Google Gemini".Official
