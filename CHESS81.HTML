    How to play

    1. The board is 9×9. Files run A B C D O E F G H; the centre file is O and the centre square O5 is the Fire Square. Red sits at the bottom and moves first.
    2. Back row, left to right: Rook, Knight, Bishop, Queen, Torch, King, Bishop, Knight, Rook. The Torch stands between the Queen and the King. The pawn in front of the Torch is the Lighter.
    3. Light your Torch: walk the Lighter onto O5. The Lighter steps one square in any direction (two straight ahead on its first move) and never captures. Lose the Lighter and the King carries the seed: your King on O5 lights the Torch instead.
    4. A match burns out: once lit, the Lighter must leave O5 within three of your moves or it is gone. When it leaves it carries on as an ordinary pawn.
    5. The Torch breathes fire. A dormant Torch cannot move and cannot be captured. A lit Torch steps one square in any direction, and after every move you make it burns enemy pieces standing next to it.
    6. Fire grows as the enemy's assistants fall. Each enemy Rook, Bishop or Knight you take or burn adds one to your Fire. Spark (lit): burns pawns. Blaze (Fire 2): also Knights and Bishops. Inferno (Fire 3 of 6): also Rooks and the Queen. Fire never touches a King.
    7. The King is the strongest piece: it steps one square any way or leaps like a Knight. The Queen moves like a chess Queen but at most 3 squares. Rooks, Bishops, Knights and pawns move as in chess; pawns promote to a Queen on the last rank. No castling, no en passant.

    How a winner is declared

    • Checkmate wins. Put the enemy King in check with no way out. CHECK. FIRE. MATE.
    • Capture the enemy Torch and you win at once. Lighting your Torch gives you fire, but a lit Torch can be taken, so guard it.
    • When the game stalls, Fire decides. Stalemate, 50 moves each with no capture or pawn move, the same position three times, or only Kings and Torches left: the side with the higher Fire wins. Equal Fire is a draw.
    • STARKS: beat the Machine as Red to earn STARKS for the Billboard: Squire +60, Knight +200, King +500 (up to five wins a day). Take-backs are only allowed at Squire level and in friend games.
