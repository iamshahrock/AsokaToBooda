▾ HOW TO PLAY CHESS 81

  1. Chess81 is played on a 9×9 board. RED moves first.
  2. Reach O5 with your Fire to ignite your Torch.
  3. Your Queen moves a maximum of 3 squares in any direction.
  4. Each fallen Rook, Bishop or Knight raises your Fire level.
  5. Win by checkmating the enemy King or capturing the enemy Torch.
