Frequently asked questions

The full FAQ is being written. Here are the quick answers to start with.

How many posters can I make?

Up to five a day per person. The limit resets at midnight, Mumbai time.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is sent only to make your poster, together with the film's poster and the KING crown. Your finished poster stays on your device until you download or share it.

What are STARKS?

STARKS are the campaign's fan points. You earn them by making posters, clearing King's Lairs, sharing and visiting. They are for fun and bragging rights, with no cash value.

How do I get on the Billboard?

Open the Billboard, choose a name and press Join. Only your chosen name and your STARKS total are published.

My STARKS disappeared. Why?

STARKS are kept in your browser on this device. Clearing your browser data or switching phones starts a fresh balance.

How do I unlock the next lair?

Clear the lair before it. The Throne Room opens once the first five are cleared.
