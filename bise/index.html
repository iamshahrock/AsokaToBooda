The engine behind Act As SRK

B.I.S.E.

Backward Integrated Script Intelligence

Powers Act As SRK

A film is the end of a long road that starts with a script. B.I.S.E. walks that road backwards: it reads a Shah Rukh Khan film, scene by scene, back into its script, then maps every character, every emotion and every shot.

  1. 1 · The script, rebuiltEvery scene found and named: place, day or night, who is in it and what happens.
  2. 2 · The character arcWho carries the story, scene by scene: screen time, share of the dialogue and the pairs who share the most scenes.
  3. 3 · The emotion arcThe emotional temperature of the film, from the tensest scene to the most uplifting.
  4. 4 · The shot composition arcClose-up, mid or wide; light, colour and cutting pace, and how they move with the story.
  5. 5 · The performanceHis voice, his eyes, his body and the signature moments, studied frame by frame.
  6. 6 · The reportOne intelligence report per film and per character, for fans, students and wannabe actors.

The first film in the lab is Deewana (1992), his first film release, starting with his entry as Raja. Studies are our own analysis; the films stay with their makers.
