Diwali 2007, again

On 9 November 2007, Diwali, Shah Rukh Khan's Om Shanti Om and Ranbir Kapoor's debut film Saawariya opened on the same day. Fifteen years later, in Brahmāstra (2022), Shah Rukh Khan walked into Ranbir Kapoor's film for a cameo. Now they share a festive season once more, 49 days apart: Ranbir as Ram at Diwali, Shah Rukh as KING at Christmas.

This time, nobody has to choose.
