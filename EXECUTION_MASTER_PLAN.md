## 8. CONTENT / RIGHTS LAYER

Before public release of any specific film artwork pack:
- confirm source and permission status
- retain provenance
- identify whether image is licensed, public, user-supplied, or otherwise approved
- do not represent fan output as official
- add takedown/contact mechanism where appropriate

User-uploaded photos:
- explicit consent
- clear privacy statement
- retention policy
- deletion path
- no unnecessary permanent storage in MVP

## 13. DEFINITION OF DONE

The project is not "done" because a webpage exists.

It is done for PUBLIC BETA when:
- a stranger can understand the idea in under 15 seconds
- a stranger can create a poster without instructions
- the poster generation path is secure
- the challenge works
- the thesis is accessible
- the site clearly distinguishes fact, recollection and proposal
- user privacy is addressed
- rights are addressed
- the experience can be measured
- failures have a recovery path
