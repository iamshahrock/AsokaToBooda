01 · Why the fans revolt

INQUILAAB, KING KE SAATH!
#FreeMyFace #FreeMyVoice #FreeMyNameA Real Revolution By Fans Begins

The makers of AI generators have built and trained models using human creativity, celebrity faces and performances without seeking permission from the people whose identities they exploit. Now they are monetising those faces, turning actors into raw material for machines and fans into unwilling spectators of an AI-generated free-for-all.

No rules. No rights. No permission. Just profit.

This anthem is the fans’ answer. No bullets. No bloodshed. Just pens, voices and art.

The demand is simple: respect the actor’s face, give fans their due, and make the face-sellers pay. No technology company should get a free pass simply because its product is powered by AI.

From every corner of the world, one voice rises in defiance:

THE KING’S FACE. THE FANS’ VOICE. EVERY ARTIST’S RIGHT.

This is an open-source Fan Anthem. Use it freely. Create your own audio or video version. Add your voice, your art, your language, and submit it.

04 · The analysis

Discovery of the new oil

When oil was discovered, it did not just fuel lamps. It created cars, roads, mechanics, inventors and a whole new playground. A star's likeness is the new oil, and today it is being drilled in the dark. The fix is not to ban the drilling. It is to build the reserve properly, with the star on board. Interpretation

The wellA licensed reserve of the star: likeness, voice, looks, characters, archive. Given with consent.
→
The refineriesAI companies turn it into licensed models and tools.
→
The carsCreator tools: poster makers, trailer kits, story engines.
→
The driversFans and storytellers, everywhere.
→
The roadsYouTube, Instagram, X, Facebook.

↺ Every use pays a share back to the well. The star earns from the fans' creativity instead of fighting it, and fans create without fear.

The fans' demands: an AI law for fans

ConsentThe star decides what is in the reserve and what is not.
ShareEvery licensed use pays the star and the IP owner.
LabelsOfficial and fan-made work clearly marked. Nobody fooled.
LimitsNo deceptive deepfakes, no harmful or explicit use.
CreditFan creators named, and able to earn from their work.

INQUILAAB is a fan movement and a petition, not a lawsuit, and it does not speak for Shah Rukh Khan or any studio. It calls for a fair framework, not for anyone to stop creating. The fans' charge of "face theft" is their framing of how AI tools use a star's likeness without consent; no specific company is accused of breaking the law. Fan works are linked to their creators' own posts and never re-hosted. #FreeMyFace
