01 · The difference

What visual AI does to a face, and what we do instead

When a star's face is the prompt

  1. It takes the faceThe tool paints a likeness it was never given permission to use.
  2. It changes the faceNew age, new body, new expression, words he never said.
  3. It hides the makingNothing in the picture tells a viewer it was made by AI.
  4. It keeps the valueThe star gets no say, no credit and no share; neither does the fan who made it.

Interpretation: the fans' account of how face-generating tools are used today. No company is accused of breaking the law.

Inside our poster studio

  1. No star's face goes inThe film poster is sent blurred, for its colour and layout only. Style editions are sent with every face masked.
  2. The rule is written into the promptThe AI is told not to depict or resemble any real actor, celebrity or public figure.
  3. The only real faces are the fans'You upload your own photo and confirm you have permission for anyone else in it.
  4. Every poster is labelledA visible "AI-MADE FAN POSTER" mark, plus AI-provenance data inside the file.
  5. Your photo is not keptIt is sent only to paint the poster.

Fact: how agarmainkinghota.com/create/ works today.
