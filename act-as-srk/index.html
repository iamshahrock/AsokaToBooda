How Match the King works

The aim: play Raja's scene the way Shah Rukh Khan played it. The score measures how close your take comes to his choices, not how good an actor you are.

  1. Pick a scene and watch it once or twice. Learn the lines, or speak your own words in his rhythm.
  2. Start camera, then Record. After 3-2-1 his scene starts beside your camera, muted, and you act along with it. Recording stops when his scene ends. Stop ends it early.
  3. Follow the cue strip under your camera:
    Your line Raja speaks: you speak.
    Others speak · listen another character talks: stay silent, listen in character.
    Raja on screen · react his face is on screen: your face is compared with his.
    Off screen: nothing is scored, stay in character.
  4. What is measured: Timing (you speak when he speaks), Speaking length, Pauses, Head movement (where he turns), Expression (how his face changes) and, in dialogue scenes, Voice (how his loudness rises and falls). Scenes without dialogue are scored on head and face only.
  5. Your card: every scene you play wins its card. Match under 50 = Bronze, 50+ = Silver, 70+ = Gold, 85+ = Diamond. Play again to upgrade it; it never goes down. Finish an act for its duo card, all scenes for Hum Teeno.
  6. Tips: face the camera, good light on your face, alone in the frame, quiet room. Your video never leaves your device.
