About us

KING by FANS: the fan-built campaign for KING, all the way to 24 December 2026.

Agar Main King Hota asks every fan one question: if you were the King, what would your story be?

Here you can make a KING poster with your own face and your own line, play the six King's Lairs, light the Torch in Chess81, follow the daily KING Buzz, and read Asoka to Boo-Da. Everything you do earns STARKS, the campaign's fan points, and the best fans rise on the Billboard.
