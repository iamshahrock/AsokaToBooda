About us

A fan campaign for KING, built by fans, for fans, all the way to 24 December 2026.

Agar Main King Hota asks every fan one question: if you were the King, what would your story be?

Here you can step into any of Shah Rukh Khan's films and make a KING poster with your own face and your own line, play the six King's Lairs, light the Torch in Chess81, follow the daily KING Buzz, and read Asoka to Boo-Da, a fan's marketing thesis on the King's journey.

Everything you do earns STARKS, the campaign's fan points, and the best fans rise on the Billboard.

Who we are

We are fans. Shah Rock Jaan presents; the campaign is created and managed by fans who love you, KING.

Fair play

Posters are made fresh for each fan from their own photo. Photos are used only to make your poster. STARKS are points for fun, with no cash value.
