How Rapid Fire works

  1. Scan: find the BLUE King. The gold outline marks the squares around him.
  2. Calculate: look for checks. Boards 1 to 6 are mate in 1, boards 7 to 15 are mate in 2.
  3. Execute: tap a RED piece, then its square. In mate in 2 the Machine answers your first move.
  4. Mate: checkmate the King before the 81 seconds run out. The seconds you have left are your points.

Same pieces and rules as the full Chess81 game: the Queen moves up to 3 squares, the King also jumps like a Knight, a lit Torch burns the enemy pieces next to it. Every board was checked by the Chess81 engine and has exactly one winning first move.
