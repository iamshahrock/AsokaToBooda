CHESS 81 MASTER DESIGN
HOW TO PLAY CHESS81
  1. Chess81 is played on a 9×9 board. RED moves first.
  2. The familiar pieces move in the familiar way. Chess81 adds a Torch and a Lighter.
  3. Move your Lighter to e5. At e5 it ignites your Torch.
  4. Once lit, the Torch moves one square like a King and becomes royal: it cannot be left in attack.
  5. The Queen is deliberately reduced: maximum 3 squares per move in any direction.
  6. Your six officers — 2 Rooks, 2 Bishops and 2 Knights — are your Fire fuel.
  7. 1 officer down: Fire 1 — adjacent enemy Pawns can burn.
  8. 2 officers down: Fire 2 — adjacent enemy Pawns, Rooks, Bishops and Knights can burn.
  9. 3 officers down: Fire 3 — the Queen becomes vulnerable to the Torch's fire.
  10. 4+ officers down: Fire 4 — the Torch creates a territorial zone around itself.
  11. The King is never burned. Win by checkmating the enemy King or capturing the enemy Torch.
  12. Playtest edition: no castling, no en passant, automatic Queen promotion.
CHECK. FIRE. MATE. is a new strategic layer built on top of familiar chess movement. The rules are intentionally being playtested before the human focus group.
HOW IS CHESS81 DIFFERENT FROM CHESS64?
CHESS648×8 · 32 pieces · one King.
CHESS819×9 · 36 pieces · King + Torch.
  1. You do not relearn the basic movement of the familiar pieces.
  2. The Lighter reaches e5 to ignite the Torch.
  3. Fallen officers increase Fire, creating a comeback mechanism.
  4. The Queen has a 3-square maximum range.
  5. At 3 officers down, Fire can burn the Queen.
  6. At 4+, Fire controls territory around the Torch.
