HOW TO PLAY
  1. Chess81 is played on a 9×9 board. RED moves first.
  2. The familiar chess pieces move as you already know them. Chess81 adds a Torch and a Lighter.
  3. Your Lighter starts on the e-file. Get it to e5 to ignite your Torch.
  4. Once lit, your Torch moves one square like a King and becomes royal: it must not be left in attack.
  5. Your Fire grows when your own Rooks, Bishops and Knights fall. Fire levels are 0–4.
  6. Fire 1 burns adjacent enemy Pawns. Fire 2 also burns enemy Rooks, Bishops and Knights. Fire 3 also burns the Queen. Fire 4 closes the King's territory around the Torch.
  7. The King is never burned. CHECK → FIRE → MATE. Win by checkmating the King or by capturing the opponent's Torch.
  8. For this playtest edition: no castling, no en passant, automatic Queen promotion.
Playtest rule set: these mechanics are deliberately provisional and are being tested before the human focus group.
HOW IS CHESS81 DIFFERENT FROM CHESS64?
CHESS648×8 · 32 pieces · White/Black · one King.
CHESS819×9 · 36 pieces · RED/BLUE · King + Torch.
  1. You do not have to relearn how the familiar pieces move.
  2. Chess81 adds one new strategic system: Fire.
  3. The Lighter reaches the centre to ignite the Torch.
  4. Your fallen officers can make your Fire stronger — creating a comeback mechanism.
  5. The Queen is no longer the only dominant power: the King, Torch and Fire change the strategic hierarchy.

The beachhead idea: if you already play Chess64, you already know most of Chess81. Learn the Torch. Learn Fire. Then play.
